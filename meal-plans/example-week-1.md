# Example Week 1: Grill Night to Sichuan Stir-Fry

> A full week for 2 adults built on leftover-chaining: every big dinner
> becomes the next day's lunch, Sunday afternoon sets up the whole week,
> and the weekend has two baking projects (because weekends should).

## Assumptions

- 2 adults; most recipes serve 4-6, so dinners deliberately make tomorrow's
  lunch. Scale up for bigger households.
- Week runs **Sunday 2026-09-20 → Saturday 2026-09-26**; shopping and prep
  happen Sunday morning.
- Saturday evening before: mix the [no-knead bread dough](../recipes/no-knead-whole-wheat-bread.md)
  (it rises overnight — bake it Sunday morning).

### Leftover chains at a glance

- Souvlaki chicken (Sun) → Mon lunch
- Chili (Mon) → Tue lunch, freeze the remainder for a future week
- Kung pao (Wed) → Thu lunch
- Tom kha (Thu) → Fri lunch
- Adobo (Fri) → Sat lunch (it genuinely improves by day two)
- Rice (Sun) → Tue dinner; fresh batch Wednesday

## Sunday prep list (~90 minutes active)

1. **Bake the bread** — the no-knead dough mixed Saturday night goes into
   the Dutch oven first thing; it bakes while you shop.
2. **Bake the banana bread** — 15 minutes hands-on; cool, slice, freeze
   half for Friday.
3. **Make the tzatziki** — 15 minutes; it improves by dinner.
4. **Make the Tuscan soup** — the full pot; portion for Wed/Thu lunches
   and refrigerate.
5. **Cook a big pot of rice** — souvlaki tonight, miso salmon Tuesday.
6. **Marinate the souvlaki chicken** (30 minutes before grilling) —
   marinate enough for tonight + tomorrow's lunch.
7. **Freezer check** — breakfast burritos: if you don't have a stash,
   make a batch while the kitchen's warm, or buy them ready.

## Grocery list

*Check the pantry and freezer before shopping — spice rack items
especially. Quantities assume nothing on hand except salt, pepper, and
basic oil/vinegar.*

### Produce
- Yellow onions ×6, garlic ×2 heads, ginger (large knob)
- Lemongrass ×2, makrut lime leaves (small pack), galangal (or extra ginger)
- Limes ×5, lemons ×3
- English cucumbers ×3, radishes ×1 bunch
- Cherry tomatoes ×2 pints
- Red bell peppers ×4, bok choy ×4 heads, sugar snap peas 200 g
- Kale ×1 bunch, carrots ×3, celery ×2 stalks
- Scallions ×2 bunches
- Bananas ×6 (as ripe as possible — ask for the discount speckled ones)
- Apples ~1.3 kg: half Honeycrisp/Fuji, half Granny Smith (pie)
- Cilantro, mint, dill, parsley ×1 bunch each
- Bird's eye chilies ×2, 1 habanero (moqueca)

### Meat & fish
- Boneless chicken thighs ~1.5 kg total (souvlaki 600 g, kung pao 450 g,
  tom kha 450 g)
- Ground turkey (dark meat) 1.4 kg / 3 lb (chili — serves 8 for a reason)
- Salmon fillets ×4 (~600 g)
- Firm white fish 800 g — **buy Saturday morning**, fresh for moqueca

### Dairy & eggs
- Eggs ×18 (shakshuka 6, banana bread 2, rolls 2, pie 1, breakfasts ~7)
- Greek yogurt 1 kg (tzatziki, banana bread, breakfasts)
- Labneh or extra-thick yogurt 500 g (Sunday platter)
- Cream cheese 115 g (roll glaze), feta (optional souvlaki/shakshuka)
- Butter 400 g (pie, rolls, toast), whole milk 500 ml (roll dough)
- Parmesan + a rind (Tuscan soup)

### Bakery & baking
- White whole wheat flour 500 g, whole wheat flour 400 g
- All-purpose flour 1 kg, bread flour 200 g
- Instant yeast, walnuts 80 g, peanuts 45 g (roasted, unsalted)
- Whole wheat tortillas ×6 + salsa (if making the burrito batch)

### Canned & jarred
- Crushed tomatoes ×3, diced tomatoes ×1
- Cannellini beans ×2 cans, black beans ×1 can
- Coconut milk ×2 cans (tom kha, moqueca)
- Chipotles in adobo + dried ancho ×3, guajillo ×2, pasilla ×2 (chili)
- Miso paste (salmon)

### Pantry & dry goods
- Jasmine rice 500 g, long-grain rice 200 g
- Chicken broth 2 L, fish sauce, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine
- Black vinegar (Chinkiang), rice vinegar, sesame oil
- Sichuan peppercorns, dried red chilies (kung pao)
- Honey, maple syrup, brown + white sugar, cornstarch
- Cinnamon, nutmeg, oregano, cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder
- Olive oil, coconut oil, neutral oil

## Flex notes

- Too many lunches out? The chili that would feed Tuesday lunch goes
  straight to the freezer — it's a freezer legend.
- Want less baking? Store-bought bread and rolls work; keep the pie.
- Moqueca swap: the salmon does fine in it if you'd rather not buy a
  second fish.
- Next week's seeds: the freezer holds chili and burritos; adobo freezes
  beautifully too.
