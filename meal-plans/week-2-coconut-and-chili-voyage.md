# Week 2: Coconut & Chili Voyage

> A warm, fragrant week that hops from the Caribbean to Brazil with a
> stop on the Thai-Ethiopian spice road. Every dinner earns its leftover
> lunch, and the weekend closes with a showy fish stew and a slice of pie.

## Assumptions

- 2 adults; most recipes serve 4-6, so dinners deliberately make tomorrow's
  lunch. Scale up for bigger households.
- Week runs **Sunday 2026-09-27 → Saturday 2026-10-03**; shopping and prep
  happen Sunday morning.
- Saturday evening before: mix the [no-knead bread dough](../recipes/no-knead-whole-wheat-bread.md)
  so it's ready to bake Sunday morning.

## Sunday prep list (~90 minutes active)

1. **Bake the bread** — the no-knead dough mixed Saturday night goes into
   the Dutch oven first thing; it bakes while you shop.
2. **Bake the banana bread** — 15 minutes hands-on; cool, slice, freeze half
   for Friday.
3. **Make the smashed cucumber salad** — it keeps for a day; drain before serving.
4. **Marinate the jerk chicken** (30 minutes before cooking) — marinate enough
   for tonight + tomorrow's lunch.
5. **Cook a big pot of rice** — jerk chicken tonight, miso salmon Monday.
6. **Freezer burrito check** — if you don't have a stash of black bean
   burritos, make a batch while the kitchen's warm.
7. **Toast the chi** — blend the golden milk spice mix and the jerk seasoning
   so Wednesday and Sunday just need a stir.

## Grocery list

*Check the pantry and freezer before shopping — spice rack items especially.
Quantities assume nothing on hand except salt, pepper, and basic oil/vinegar.*

### Produce
- Yellow onions ×6, garlic ×2 heads, ginger (large knob)
- Scallions ×2 bunches, bird's-eye chilies ×3
- English cucumbers ×3, radishes ×1 bunch (salad)
- Bell peppers ×3 (green + red), bok choy ×4 heads, sugar snap peas 200 g
- Limes ×5, lemons ×4
- Cherry tomatoes ×2 pints, cilantro ×2 bunches, thai basil ×1 bunch, parsley ×1
- Kale or spinach ×1 bunch, carrots ×4, celery ×3 stalks
- Bananas ×6 (ripe — ask for the speckled ones)
- Apples ~1.3 kg for the pie (mix Honeycrisp + Granny Smith)
- Avocado ×2 (mousse), sweet potatoes ×2 (if serving with miso)

### Meat & fish
- Boneless chicken thighs ~1.5 kg total (jerk 600 g, kung pao 450 g,
  thai green curry 450 g)
- Chicken thighs for adobo ~700 g
- Salmon fillets ×4 (~600 g)
- Firm white fish 800 g for moqueca — **buy Saturday morning**, fresh

### Dairy & eggs
- Eggs ×20 (shakshuka 6, benedict 8, biscuits 1, banana bread 2, breakfasts ~5)
- Greek yogurt 1 kg (banana bread, breakfasts, sauces)
- Labneh or extra-thick yogurt 500 g (Sunday platter)
- Butter 350 g (pie, biscuits, benedict, toast), whole milk 500 ml
- Parmesan (optional, over misir wot)

### Bakery & baking
- White whole wheat flour 500 g, all-purpose flour 1 kg
- Instant yeast, brown + white sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg
- Whole wheat tortillas ×6 + salsa (if making the burrito batch)

### Canned & jarred
- Crushed tomatoes ×3, diced tomatoes ×1
- Black beans ×2 cans, red lentils ×2 bags (500 g)
- Coconut milk ×2 cans (thai curry, moqueca)
- Miso paste (salmon), fish sauce, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine
- Black vinegar (Chinkiang), rice vinegar, sesame oil

### Pantry & dry goods
- Jasmine rice 500 g
- Chicken broth 2 L, tomato paste, peanut oil
- Sichuan peppercorns, dried red chilies (kung pao), jerk spice blend
- Chorizo or smoked paprika (curry), cornstarch
- Honey, maple syrup, olive oil, coconut oil, neutral oil

## Flex notes

- Too much heat for a midweek? The jerk marinade is the only really spicy
  item — mellow it with extra lime and a pinch of brown sugar on Sunday.
- Miso salmon swap: if you'd rather not buy bok choy, broccoli or green beans
  roast on the same sheet pan just as well.
- Moqueca without the extra fish: the salmon does fine in it if you'd rather
  not buy a second fish.
- Short on time Wednesday? Canned lentils + a splash of the curry paste make a
  quick stand-in for the misir wot.
- Next week's seeds: the freezer holds bulgogi-ready rice and black bean
  burritos; adobo and misir wot both freeze beautifully.
