# Week 3: Mediterranean & Moroccan Braise

> A slow-food week built around braises, beans, and bread. Greek grilling
> opens the week, Moroccan and Indian pots keep it cozy, and the weekend
> ends with Thai basil chicken and fresh cinnamon rolls.

## Assumptions

- 2 adults; most recipes serve 4-6, so dinners deliberately make tomorrow's
  lunch. Scale up for bigger households.
- Week runs **Sunday 2026-10-04 → Saturday 2026-10-10**; shopping and prep
  happen Sunday morning.
- Bake the [olive oil citrus cake](../recipes/olive-oil-citrus-cake.md) and the
  [rosemary focaccia](../recipes/rosemary-focaccia.md) on Sunday so the week has
  bread and a dessert on hand.

## Sunday prep list (~90 minutes active)

1. **Bake the focaccia** — 40 minutes with long rises; bake it first so it's
   out of the oven before you get to the rest.
2. **Bake the olive oil citrus cake** — it keeps well all week and doubles as
   breakfast with coffee.
3. **Make the tzatziki** — 15 minutes; it improves by dinner.
4. **Roast the crispy chickpeas** — a snack for Sunday and midweek.
5. **Marinate the souvlaki chicken** — enough for tonight + tomorrow's lunch.
6. **Make the Moroccan stew** — the full pot; portion for Tue/Wed and refrigerate.
7. **Freezer burrito check** — top up the black bean burrito stash if needed.
8. **Cook a big pot of rice** — chana masala Wednesday.

## Grocery list

*Check the pantry and freezer before shopping — spice rack items especially.
Quantities assume nothing on hand except salt, pepper, and basic oil/vinegar.*

### Produce
- Yellow onions ×6, garlic ×2 heads, ginger (large knob)
- Lemons ×4, oranges ×2 (citrus cake), limes ×3
- English cucumber ×2, cherry tomatoes ×2 pints
- Red bell peppers ×3, sweet potatoes ×2 (large)
- Kale ×1 bunch, carrots ×4, celery ×2 stalks, spinach ×1 bunch

- Cilantro ×2 bunches, mint ×1, parsley ×1, thai basil ×1 (pad krapow)
- Scallions ×2 bunches, bird's-eye chilies ×2, chili flakes

- Beef (flank or sirloin) 450 g for bulgogi bowls

- Cream cheese 115 g (roll glaze), whole milk 500 ml (roll dough, cake)

### Bakery & baking
- White whole wheat flour 500 g, all-purpose flour 1 kg, bread flour 200 g
- Instant yeast, rolled oats 100 g (snack cake)
- Walnuts or almonds 100 g, brown + white sugar, honey, maple syrup
- Cinnamon, nutmeg, cumin, turmeric, smoked paprika, coriander, garam masala

### Canned & jarred
- Canned chickpeas ×3, cannellini beans ×2, black beans ×2
- Crushed tomatoes ×3, coconut milk ×1 (optional, chana/curry)
- Chipotle in adobo (ping), tomato paste
- Kalamata olives 150 g (sheet-pan), tahini (optional)

### Pantry & dry goods
- Jasmine rice 500 g, chicken or veg broth 2 L
- Soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, fish sauce
- Gochujang (bulgogi), dried chilies, Sichuan peppercorns
- Olive oil, neutral oil, dried oregano, bay leaf

## Flex notes

- Want less baking? Store-bought focaccia, bread, and rolls all work — keep
  the citrus cake, it's the weekend payoff.
- Bulgogi beef swap: thinly sliced chicken thighs or mushrooms work great and
  are cheaper.
- Moroccan stew upsize: it freezes brilliantly, so double it for a future week.
- Too many stews midweek? Grill a couple of the marinated souvlaki thighs on
  Tuesday and save the sheet-pan dinner for a night you want to.do nothing.
- Next week's seeds: the freezer holds bulgogi-ready rice, chili, and sticky
  cinnamon roll glaze; bulgogi and chana masala both freeze beautifully.
