# Week 4: Cozy Soups & Comfort Bowls

> A week that leans into one-pot comfort: chicken pot pie, a big chili, and a
> rotation of soups from Senegal to Peru. Chicken stays the protein when you
> want it, and the freezer does the heavy lifting.

## Assumptions

- 2 adults; most recipes serve 4-6, so dinners deliberately make tomorrow's
  lunch. Scale up for bigger households.
- Week runs **Sunday 2026-10-11 → Saturday 2026-10-17**; shopping and prep
  happen Sunday morning.
- Saturday evening before: mix the [no-knead bread dough](../recipes/no-knead-whole-wheat-bread.md)
  so it's ready to bake Sunday morning.

### Leftover chains at a glance

- Pot pie (Sun) → Mon lunch
- Chili (Mon) → Tue lunch, freeze the remainder for a future week
- Nasi goreng (Tue) → Wed lunch
- Pan-seared chicken (Wed) → Thu lunch
- Peanut soup (Thu) → Fri lunch

- Cooked rice (Sun) → nasi goreng Tuesday; make extra while the pot's hot

## Sunday prep list (~90 minutes active)

1. **Bake the bread** — the no-knead dough mixed Saturday night bakes while
   you shop; it carries the Saturday breakfast too.
2. **Bake the banana bread** — 15 minutes hands-on; cool, slice, freeze half
   for Monday/Friday.
3. **Make the chicken pot pie** (or prep the whole filling) — assemble it
   Sunday so Monday only needs reheating.
4. **Make the turkey chili** — the big batch; it feeds Monday lunch and
   freezes beautifully.
5. **Roast the crispy chickpeas** — a snack for Sunday and midweek.
6. **Cook a big pot of rice** — nasi goreng benefits from day-old rice, so
   cook extra on Sunday.
7. **Freezer burrito check** — top up the black bean burrito stash if needed.
8. **Make golden milk spice mix** — ready to whisk into any breakfast.

## Grocery list

*Check the pantry and freezer before shopping — spice rack items especially.
Quantities assume nothing on hand except salt, pepper, and basic oil/vinegar.*

### Produce
- Yellow onions ×6, garlic ×2 heads, ginger (large knob)
- Celery ×4 stalks, carrots ×5, potatoes ~500 g (pot pie)
- Kale or spinach ×1 bunch, mixed greens ×2 bags (lunches)
- Cherry tomatoes ×1 pint, lemon ×2, lime ×3
- Scallions ×2 bunches, thai basil (tom kha with the curry), cilantro ×1
- Red bell pepper ×1, button mushrooms 250 g (nasi goreng)
- Bananas ×6 (ripe), avocado ×2 (mousse)
- Sweet potatoes ×2 (I like them roasted with the chili)

### Meat & fish
- Whole chicken or thighs ~1.2 kg (pot pie 600 g, chili uses ground)
- Ground turkey (dark meat) 1.4 kg / 3 lb — chili, serves 8 for a reason
- Boneless skinless chicken breast ×4 (pan-seared) + 450 g for tom kha
- Chicken broth 2 L

### Dairy & eggs
- Eggs ×18 (benedict 8, shakshuka 6, biscuits 1, banana bread 2, breakfasts ~5)
- Greek yogurt 1 kg (banana bread, labneh platter, sauces)
- Labneh or extra-thick yogurt 500 g (Friday platter)
- Butter 350 g (biscuits, pot pie top, toast), whole milk 500 ml
- Cream 250 ml (tom kha), heavy cream 100 ml (optional ice cream)

### Bakery & baking
- White whole wheat flour 500 g, all-purpose flour 1 kg
- Instant yeast, brown + white sugar, honey, maple syrup
- Cinnamon, nutmeg, smoked paprika, cumin, oregano
- Whole wheat tortillas ×6 + salsa (burrito stash)

### Canned & jarred
- Crushed tomatoes ×3, diced tomatoes ×2, tomato paste
- Cannellini beans ×2, black beans ×2, white beans ×1
- Peanut butter (natural) 1 jar — the peanut soup's backbone
- Coconut milk ×3 (peanut soup, tom kha), fish sauce, soy sauce
- Chipotles in adobo + dried ancho/guajillo/pasilla ×3 (chili)

### Pantry & dry goods
- Quinoa 300 g, jasmine rice 500 g
- Chicken broth 2 L, red lentils 250 g (optional soup boost)
- Neutral oil, sesame oil, rice vinegar, peanut oil
- Ginger, garlic paste, golden milk spices (turmeric, black pepper, cardamom)
- Vanilla extract, cocoa powder (mousse), cornstarch (sauce)

## Flex notes

- Too much soup by Thursday? Swap the Peruvian soup for a lighter green
  salad + avocado toast made from Sunday's bread.
- Chili upsize: it's the freezer hero — double it and you've got next week's
  lunches covered too.
- Peanut soup swap: try it with extra sweet potato and a squeeze of lime if
  you want it brighter.
- Nasi goreng needs day-old rice, so plan to cook it on Sunday and again
  midweek if it disappears.
- Next week's seeds: the freezer holds chili, burritos, and a cooked chicken
  carcass for stock; peanut soup and quinoa soup freeze terrifically.
