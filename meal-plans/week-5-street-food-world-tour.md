# Week 5: Street-Food World Tour

> A globetrotting, mostly-meat-free week of big-flavor soups and bowls: a
> Caribbean coconut pot, Sichuan mapo tofu, a Vietnamese noodle bowl, and a
> trio of lentil soups from Morocco, the Mediterranean, and beyond.

## Assumptions

- 2 adults; most recipes serve 4-6, so dinners deliberately make tomorrow's
  lunch. Scale up for bigger households.
- Week runs **Sunday 2026-10-18 → Saturday 2026-10-24**; shopping and prep
  happen Sunday morning.
- Saturday evening before: feed the [everyday sourdough starter](../recipes/everyday-sourdough-boule.md)
  so it's ready to mix and bake Sunday morning. (If you don't keep a starter,
  the [no-knead bread](../recipes/no-knead-whole-wheat-bread.md) makes a fine
  stand-in.)

## Sunday prep list (~90 minutes active)

1. **Bake the sourdough** — mix Saturday night, bake in the Dutch oven first
   thing Sunday; it's Sunday snack, Saturday breakfast, and toast all week.
2. **Bake the banana bread** — 15 minutes hands-on; cool, slice, freeze half.
3. **Make the Caribbean coconut soup** — the full pot; portion for Sun/Mon.
4. **Make the black bean chili** — big batch; it covers Tuesday lunch and
   freezes like a champ.
5. **Roast the crispy chickpeas** — a snack for Sunday and midweek.
6. **Bake the rosemary focaccia** — for breakfast Wednesday.
7. **Make the smashed cucumber salad** — drain and dress fresh on Tuesday.
8. **Freezer burrito check** — top up the black bean burrito stash.

## Grocery list

*Check the pantry and freezer before shopping — spice rack items especially.
Quantities assume nothing on hand except salt, pepper, and basic oil/vinegar.*

### Produce
- Yellow onions ×6, garlic ×2 heads, ginger (large knob)
- Sweet potatoes ×4 (small/medium — souping week)
- Carrots ×5, celery ×5 stalks, kale ×1 bunch
- Lemongrass ×2, limes ×4, lemon ×2, cilantro ×2 bunches, thai basil (optional)
- Scallions ×2 bunches, bird's-eye chilies ×2, serrano ×1
- English cucumbers ×2, radishes ×1 bunch (salad)
- Cabbage or mixed greens ×1 bag (lemongrass bowls)
- Bananas ×6 (ripe), blueberries or mixed berries 250 g (snack cake)
- Avocado ×2 (mousse), cherry tomatoes ×1 pint

### Meat & fish
- Boneless chicken thighs or breast ~1.2 kg (caribbean uses no meat;
  chicken noodle soup 500 g, lemongrass bowls 500 g)
- Lemongrass, fish sauce, and a fresh bird's-eye or two for the bowls

### Dairy & eggs
- Eggs ×16 (shakshuka 6, soda bread 1, focaccia 1, banana bread 2, breakfasts ~6)
- Greek yogurt 1 kg (banana bread, snack cake, breakfasts)
- Labneh or extra-thick yogurt 500 g (Sunday platter)
- Butter 300 g (toast, focaccia, cake), whole milk 300 ml
- Cream cheese + a little sugar (with the snack cake, optional)

### Bakery & baking
- Bread flour 500 g + whole wheat flour 300 g (sourdough)
- All-purpose flour 500 g, white whole wheat flour 300 g
- Instant yeast (focaccia), rolled oats 100 g (snack cake)
- Walnuts or almonds 100 g, brown + white sugar, honey, maple syrup
- Cinnamon, turmeric, cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, garam masala

### Canned & jarred
- Black beans ×4, red lentils ×3 bags (500 g each), green/brown lentils ×2
- Crushed tomatoes ×3, diced tomatoes ×1, tomato paste
- Coconut milk ×1 can (caribbean soup), chickpeas optional
- Miso paste (mapo), chili bean paste (doubanjiang), soy sauce
- Fish sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil

### Pantry & dry goods
- Jasmine rice 500 g, quinoa (optional boost)
- Veg or chicken broth 3 L (soup-heavy week — you'll go through it)
- Sichuan peppercorns, dried red chilies, cornstarch
- Olive oil, neutral oil, unsweetened cocoa (mousse)

## Flex notes

- No sourdough starter? The [no-knead bread](../recipes/no-knead-whole-wheat-bread.md)
  or a store-bought loaf does the job — just skip the Saturday feed.
- Soup fatigue by Friday? Pile the lentil soup onto toast with a poached egg
  for a heartier dinner, or swap it for a fresh salad bowl.
- Mapo tofu swap: use firm tofu and extra mushrooms to keep it vegetarian
  (the recipe already leans that way); add a splash of broth if you like it
  saucier.
- Black bean chili upsize: it's the freezer hero — double it for a future week.
- Next week's seeds: the freezer holds black bean chili, burritos, and a
  stash of cooked rice; all three lentil soups freeze beautifully.
