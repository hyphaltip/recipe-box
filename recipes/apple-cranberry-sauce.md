# Apple Cranberry Sauce

> A tart-sweet, chunky cranberry sauce simmered with diced apple and orange, ready in under half an hour and made for the holiday table.

## Ingredients
- 340 g (12 oz) fresh or frozen cranberries, picked over and rinsed
- 1 medium apple (about 170 g / 6 oz), peeled, cored, and diced 1/2-inch
- 150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) water
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) orange juice
- 1 strip orange zest, about 2 inches long
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 pinch fine salt

## Instructions
1. Combine the sugar, water, and orange juice in a medium saucepan over medium heat and stir until the sugar dissolves completely, about 3 minutes.
2. Add the cranberries, diced apple, orange zest, cinnamon stick, and salt, then bring to a gentle simmer.
3. Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until most of the cranberries have popped and the apple is tender when pierced with a knife, 12-15 minutes.
4. Press the mixture against the side of the pan with a wooden spoon to crush about half the berries, leaving some whole for texture.
5. Fish out the cinnamon stick and orange zest, then take the pan off the heat.
6. Cool to room temperature, about 1 hour, until the sauce thickens to a spoonable gel; stir once more before serving.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a less sweet sauce, drop the sugar to 100 g (1/2 cup); the apple and orange juice carry the rest.
- Swap the granulated sugar for 120 ml (1/2 cup) maple syrup for a deeper flavor (still vegan) — expect a slightly looser set.
- Replace the orange juice with water plus 1 tablespoon lemon juice if you want a sharper, more classic cranberry profile.
- Add 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger or a star anise pod with the cinnamon for a spiced variation.
- Frozen cranberries work straight from the bag; add 2-3 minutes to the simmer time.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 1 week; the flavor deepens after a day.
- Freeze for up to 3 months in a sealed container or freezer bag; thaw overnight in the fridge and stir well.
- Make up to 3 days ahead and keep chilled — serve cold or gently rewarmed over low heat, adding a splash of water if it has thickened too much.
