# Banana Bread

> A moist, classic banana bread with a tender crumb — the perfect way to use up overripe bananas.

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed (about 1½ cups / 360 g)
- ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, melted
- ¾ cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Grease a 9×5-inch loaf pan.
3. In a large bowl, mash the bananas until smooth.
4. Stir in melted butter, sugar, egg, and vanilla until well combined.
5. Sprinkle baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir in.
6. Fold in flour until just combined, being careful not to overmix.
7. Pour batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top.
8. Bake until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, 50–60 minutes.
9. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes.
10. Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Notes & Substitutions
- Lighter swap: replace half the butter with unsweetened applesauce and reduce sugar to ½ cup (100 g).
- Vegan: use 1 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tbsp water instead of the egg, and coconut oil or vegan butter instead of butter.
- Gluten-free: substitute a 1:1 gluten-free flour blend for the all-purpose flour.
- Add ½ cup chopped walnuts or chocolate chips for extra texture.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to 1 week.
- Freeze wrapped for up to 3 months; thaw at room temperature or warm in the microwave.
- Make ahead: bake up to 2 days in advance — the flavor actually improves after a day.
