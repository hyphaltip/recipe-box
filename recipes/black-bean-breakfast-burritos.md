# Freezer Black Bean Breakfast Burritos

> Protein-packed make-ahead burritos with spiced black beans and soft scrambled
> eggs — build a stash on Sunday and breakfast is a 90-second reheat all week.

## Ingredients

- 8 large eggs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced, optional
- 400 g / 14 oz can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 3/4 cup shredded cheddar or pepper jack, about 85 g
- 6 large whole wheat tortillas (25 cm / 10 inch)
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaves, optional
- 1 lime, cut in wedges
- salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat; cook the onion,
   bell pepper, and jalapeño (if using) until softened, 5-6 minutes.
2. Add the black beans, cumin, and chili powder; cook, mashing about a
   quarter of the beans, until warm and slightly saucy, 2-3 minutes. Season
   with salt and pepper and transfer to a bowl.
3. Wipe the pan, return it to medium-low heat, and add the eggs with a pinch
   of salt; stir slowly with a spatula until just set but still glossy,
   3-4 minutes. Remove from heat — they finish cooking in the wrap.
4. Warm the tortillas 15 seconds each in a dry skillet or microwave so they
   roll without cracking.
5. Divide the beans, eggs, cheese, and a spoonful of salsa down the center
   of each tortilla; fold in the sides and roll tightly.
6. Wrap each burrito in foil or parchment. Refrigerate up to 4 days or
   freeze up to 3 months.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Lighter swap: use 6 whole eggs + 4 egg whites and cut the cheese to 1/2 cup.
- Vegan: swap eggs for a firm tofu scramble and use dairy-free cheese or skip it.
- Gluten-free: use corn or GF tortillas (smaller — make 8 instead of 6).
- Reheat from the fridge: microwave in a damp paper towel, 60-90 seconds.
  From frozen: microwave 3-4 minutes, flipping halfway, or 350 °F oven
  wrapped in foil for 25 minutes.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Refrigerate up to 4 days; freeze up to 3 months (foil-wrapped, in a bag).
- Cool the filling before rolling so the tortillas don't steam and tear.
- Label with the freeze date — best within 2 months for texture.
