# Black Bean Chili

> A hearty, smoky vegetarian chili loaded with black beans and vegetables — perfect for cozy weeknight dinners and meal prep.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans (15 oz / 425 g each) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz / 411 g) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) tomato sauce
- 1 cup (240 ml) vegetable broth
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Optional: 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, minced, for extra heat
- For serving: avocado slices, fresh cilantro, lime wedges, vegan sour cream or Greek yogurt

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion and bell peppers, cook until softened, 5-7 min.
2. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, 1 min.
3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano; toast until fragrant, 1 min.
4. Add black beans, diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, vegetable broth, and chipotle (if using). Stir to combine.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 20-25 min.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Serve hot, topped with avocado, cilantro, lime wedges, and a dollop of vegan sour cream or Greek yogurt.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a vegan version, use vegan sour cream or omit the dairy topping entirely; the chili itself is naturally vegan.
- For gluten-free, ensure your chili powder and other spices are certified gluten-free (most pure spices are).
- Swap black beans for kidney beans or pinto beans for variety.
- Add 1 cup frozen corn kernels during the last 10 minutes of simmering for extra sweetness and texture.
- For a lower-sodium version, use no-salt-added canned beans and tomatoes.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
- Freeze in portioned containers for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the fridge.
- Reheat on the stovetop over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, or microwave in 1-minute intervals.
- This chili tastes even better the next day — make it ahead for easy lunches or dinners.
