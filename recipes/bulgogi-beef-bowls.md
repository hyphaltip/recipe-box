# Bulgogi Beef Bowls

> Korean marinated beef, caramelized fast in a hot pan and served over rice
> with quick pickled carrots and sesame spinach.

## Ingredients

- 500 g / 1 lb beef sirloin, very thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1.5 tbsp honey or brown sugar
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1/2 small Asian pear, grated, about 1/3 cup (or 1 tbsp extra honey + splash of apple juice)
- black pepper, to taste
- 1.5 cups short-grain or jasmine rice, cooked, for serving
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, for searing
- Sesame spinach:
  - 200 g / 7 oz baby spinach
  - 1 tsp sesame oil, 1/2 tsp soy sauce, pinch of salt
- Quick pickled carrots:
  - 2 carrots, julienned
  - 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sugar, pinch of salt
- sesame seeds and sliced scallions, to serve
- kimchi, to serve, optional

## Instructions

1. Toss the carrots with the vinegar, sugar, and salt; set aside to pickle
   while everything else cooks.
2. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, pear, and pepper.
   If the beef isn't pre-sliced thin, freeze it 20 minutes and slice as thin
   as you can across the grain. Toss with the marinade 20-30 minutes.
3. Wilt the spinach in a hot dry pan 1 minute (or pour boiling water over
   it); squeeze dry, roughly chop, and dress with sesame oil, soy sauce,
   and salt.
4. Heat the neutral oil in a large skillet over high heat until smoking;
   sear the beef in a single layer, in batches, 1-2 minutes per side until
   glossy and caramelized — don't crowd the pan.
5. Build the bowls: rice, beef, sesame spinach, pickled carrots; shower
   with sesame seeds and scallions. Kimchi on the side.

## Notes & Substitutions

- The pear isn't optional fluff — its enzymes tenderize the beef and round
  the marinade's edge. Apple works too.
- Low-carb: wrap the beef in lettuce leaves instead of rice.
- Leaner: use eye of round or flank; keep slices paper-thin so it stays tender.
- Meal-prep: the components hold beautifully — pack rice, beef, and veg
  separately and reheat the rice and beef only.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Cooked bulgogi keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat briefly in a hot pan.
- Marinated raw beef: cook within 24 hours, or freeze (in marinade) 2 months.
- Pickled carrots keep 1 week and get better.
