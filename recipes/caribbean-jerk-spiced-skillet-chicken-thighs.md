# Caribbean Jerk-Spiced Skillet Chicken Thighs

> Boneless chicken thighs rubbed with a warm, spicy-sweet jerk seasoning and
> seared until charred at the edges — big flavor from a single skillet in
> under 40 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 8 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 900 g / 2 lb)
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp ground allspice
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/4-1/2 tsp cayenne, or 1 minced Scotch bonnet, to taste
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 2 tbsp lime juice, plus wedges to serve
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced, to garnish

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the brown sugar, allspice, thyme, cinnamon, nutmeg, cayenne, garlic, ginger, lime juice, and salt into a thick paste. Rub all over the chicken thighs.
2. Marinate at room temperature for 20-30 minutes, or refrigerate up to overnight for deeper flavor.
3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken in a single layer (working in batches if needed) until deeply charred and cooked through, 5-6 minutes per side (internal temperature 74°C / 165°F).
4. Let the chicken rest 5 minutes, then slice or serve whole. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top and scatter with scallions.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Adjust the heat by scaling the cayenne or Scotch bonnet up or down — the spice blend is fully flavorful even mild.
- Bone-in, skin-on thighs work too: sear skin-side down until crisp, then finish in a 190°C / 375°F oven for 15-20 minutes to cook through without burning the spice crust.
- Serve with coconut rice and peas or a simple cabbage slaw to round out the plate.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Refrigerate cooked chicken up to 4 days in an airtight container.
- The raw, marinated chicken freezes well up to 3 months — thaw overnight in the fridge before searing.
- Reheat gently in a covered skillet with a splash of water to keep the chicken from drying out.
