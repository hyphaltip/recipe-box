# Chana Masala

> A cozy Indian chickpea curry simmered in a spiced tomato-onion sauce — weeknight comfort in a bowl.

## Ingredients
- 2 cans (15 oz / 425 g each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) diced tomatoes, with juices
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat; add onion and cook until soft and golden, 6–8 min.
2. Add garlic and ginger; cook until fragrant, about 1 min.
3. Stir in cumin and garam masala; toast until fragrant, about 30 sec.
4. Add tomatoes with their juices; cook until slightly thickened, 5–7 min.
5. Add chickpeas and salt; simmer until flavors meld and sauce thickens, 10–12 min, mashing a few chickpeas against the pot to thicken if desired.
6. Garnish with cilantro and serve hot with rice or naan.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a lighter version, use 1 tbsp oil or sauté the onion in a splash of water instead.
- Naturally vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free — no substitutions needed for those diets.
- Swap canned chickpeas for 1½ cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight and cooked until tender.
- Add a pinch of cayenne or chili flakes for heat.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
- Freeze for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the fridge.
- Reheat on the stovetop over medium-low heat, adding a splash of water if too thick.
- Make ahead: prepare the sauce base up to 2 days in advance; add chickpeas when reheating.
