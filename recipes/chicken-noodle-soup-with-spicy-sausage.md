# Chicken Noodle Soup with Spicy Sausage

> A hearty, soul-warming soup that pairs tender chicken and egg noodles with smoky, spicy sausage for a bold twist on the classic.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 8 oz spicy Italian sausage (or andouille), sliced into rounds
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and sliced
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 bay leaf
- 6 oz wide egg noodles
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or stockpot over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and cook until browned, 4–5 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
2. Add the chicken to the same pot and cook until golden on all sides, 5–6 minutes. Transfer to the plate with the sausage.
3. Add onion, carrots, and celery to the pot; sauté until softened, 5–6 minutes.
4. Stir in garlic and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds.
5. Pour in the chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Add thyme and bay leaf.
6. Return the chicken and sausage to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the chicken is cooked through, 12–15 minutes.
7. Add the egg noodles and cook until tender, 8–10 minutes.
8. Remove the bay leaf and thyme sprigs. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
9. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a lighter version, use turkey sausage and chicken breast instead of thighs.
- To make it gluten-free, swap the egg noodles for gluten-free noodles or rice noodles.
- This recipe is naturally dairy-free.
- For a creamy variation, stir in ½ cup heavy cream or coconut milk at the end.
- Add a handful of baby spinach in the last 2 minutes of cooking for extra greens.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
- Freeze for up to 3 months — best to freeze without the noodles, as they soften; add fresh noodles when reheating.
- Reheat gently on the stovetop over medium-low heat, adding a splash of broth or water if it thickens.
- Prep ahead: chop the vegetables and slice the sausage up to 2 days in advance; store in the fridge.
