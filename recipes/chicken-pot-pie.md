# Weeknight Chicken Pot Pie with Whole Wheat Biscuit Top

> All the comfort of chicken pot pie with a lighter hand: a vegetable-loaded filling thickened with broth and a modest amount of butter, capped with fluffy whole wheat drop biscuits. One skillet, one dinner, and it freezes beautifully.

## Ingredients

- 450 g / 3 cups cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie is perfect here)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 600 ml / 2.5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 180 ml / 3/4 cup whole milk
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1/2 tsp dried)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 300 g / 2 cups frozen peas and corn, no need to thaw
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- salt and black pepper to taste
- Biscuit top: 250 g / 2 cups white whole wheat flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 55 g / 4 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 240 ml / 1 cup cold buttermilk
- 1 tbsp honey

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 220°C / 425°F. Melt the olive oil and butter in a large oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrots, and celery with a pinch of salt until softened, 8–10 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and thyme and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir for 1–2 minutes to cook out the raw taste.
3. Whisk in the broth a splash at a time, then the milk, bay leaf, and Dijon. Simmer, stirring, until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon, 3–5 minutes. It should still look slightly loose — it will tighten in the oven.
4. Fold in the chicken, peas and corn, and parsley. Remove the bay leaf, season with salt and pepper, and level the surface.
5. Make the biscuits: whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl. Cut in the cold butter with your fingers or a pastry blender until the pieces are pea-sized. Stir in the buttermilk and honey with a fork just until a shaggy dough forms — don't overmix.
6. Drop the dough in 6–8 mounds over the hot filling, leaving gaps for steam.
7. Bake until the biscuits are golden and the filling bubbles at the edges, 22–28 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before serving — the filling is molten straight from the oven.

## Notes & Substitutions

- The double-cooking trick: the filling is fully cooked before baking, so the oven time only needs to set the biscuits. That's what makes this weeknight-legal.
- Swap the chicken for a 400 g can of chickpeas (drained) plus an extra carrot to make it vegetarian — use vegetable broth.
- Any 2 cups of mixed vegetables work: green beans, corn, diced parsnip, or leftover roasted vegetables folded in at the end.
- Whole milk keeps the sauce light but silky; evaporated milk is a fine stand-in.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Assemble through step 4 and refrigerate up to 24 hours; top with biscuit dough just before baking (add 5 minutes).
- Freeze the assembled, unbaked pie (without biscuits) up to 3 months. Bake from thawed, topping with fresh biscuits.
- Leftovers keep 3 days refrigerated; the biscuits soften — revive them briefly in a hot oven.
