# Greek Chicken Souvlaki Bowls

> Lemon-garlic-oregano chicken over rice with a crunchy cucumber-tomato salad
> and a big spoonful of tzatziki — like a Greek taverna, in bowl form.

## Ingredients

- 600 g / 1.3 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut in 3 cm / 1.5 inch pieces
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 3 tbsp lemon juice, about 1.5 lemons
- 4 garlic cloves, grated or minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 3/4 cup long-grain rice, or 2 cups cooked
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 250 g / 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 recipe [Tzatziki](tzatziki.md), or 1 cup store-bought
- kalamata olives and crumbled feta, to serve, optional
- lemon wedges, to serve

## Instructions

1. Whisk 2 tbsp olive oil, the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and
   pepper; toss with the chicken and marinate 20-30 minutes while you prep
   everything else.
2. Cook the rice (or warm cooked rice) and set aside, covered.
3. Toss the cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion with the vinegar, remaining
   1 tbsp oil, and a pinch of salt.
4. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high; cook the chicken in a
   single layer, turning occasionally, until charred in spots and cooked
   through, 10-12 minutes (165 °F / 74 °C inside). Rest 3 minutes.
5. Build the bowls: rice, cucumber salad, chicken, a generous spoonful of
   tzatziki, and olives and feta if using. Finish with lemon wedges.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Thighs stay juiciest, but breast works — pull it off the heat a few
  minutes sooner.
- Outdoor grill: thread on skewers, 10-12 minutes turning often.
- Low-carb: skip the rice and serve over shredded romaine or cauliflower rice.
- Not dairy-free if you add feta — it's optional. Tzatziki can be swapped
  for hummus to keep it fully dairy-free.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Components keep 4 days refrigerated, assembled separately; assemble
  just before eating.
- Marinate raw chicken up to 24 hours — great next-day flavor.
- Marinated chicken freezes 2 months; thaw overnight before cooking.
