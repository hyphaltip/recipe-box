# Classic Chicken Noodle Soup

> A soul-warming, one-pot classic with tender shredded chicken, hearty vegetables, and egg noodles — perfect for cold nights or when you need a comforting bowl.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 carrots, peeled and sliced
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 lbs (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 8 cups (1.9 L) low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 8 oz (225 g) wide egg noodles
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery; cook until softened, 6-8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds.
3. Add chicken, broth, bay leaves, and thyme. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until chicken is cooked through, 20-25 minutes.
4. Remove chicken from pot and shred with two forks.
5. Add egg noodles to the pot and cook until al dente, 8-10 minutes.
6. Return shredded chicken to the pot. Season with salt and pepper. Discard bay leaves.
7. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a lighter version, use skinless chicken breast and low-sodium broth.
- For gluten-free: substitute gluten-free egg noodles or rice noodles.
- This recipe is naturally dairy-free.
- Add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice before serving for brightness.
- Use rotisserie chicken to save time; add it in step 5 with the noodles.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
- Freeze for up to 3 months (without noodles, as they become mushy; add fresh noodles when reheating).
- Reheat on the stovetop over medium-low heat, adding a splash of broth or water if needed.
- Prep-ahead: chop vegetables and store in the fridge up to 2 days ahead.
