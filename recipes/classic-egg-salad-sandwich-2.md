# Classic Egg Salad Sandwich

> A creamy, tangy lunchbox classic that comes together in under 30 minutes — perfect for picnics, meal prep, and quick weeknight dinners.

## Ingredients
- 8 large eggs
- 1/2 cup (120 g) mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp (30 g) Dijon mustard
- 1/2 cup finely diced celery, about 1 stalk
- 1/4 cup finely diced red onion
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh dill, or 1 tsp dried dill
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 tsp paprika, for garnish (optional)
- 8 slices sandwich bread, or 4 buns/rolls
- 4 lettuce leaves, for serving (optional)

## Instructions
1. Place eggs in a medium saucepan and cover with cold water by 1 inch; bring to a boil over high heat.
2. Remove from heat, cover, and let stand 10–12 minutes until fully cooked.
3. Drain eggs and transfer to an ice water bath; cool 5 minutes, then peel.
4. Chop peeled eggs into 1/4-inch pieces and place in a medium bowl.
5. Add mayonnaise, mustard, celery, red onion, dill, salt, and pepper; stir gently until creamy and well combined.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning with more salt, pepper, or mustard as desired.
7. Divide egg salad evenly among 4 slices of bread, top with remaining slices, and serve immediately.

## Notes & Substitutions
- Lighter swap: Replace half the mayonnaise with plain Greek yogurt to cut calories and boost protein (not dairy-free).
- Vegan option: Use vegan mayonnaise and swap the eggs for 1 (14 oz) block of firm tofu, crumbled and seasoned with 1/2 tsp turmeric for color.
- Gluten-free: Serve on gluten-free bread or wrap in large lettuce leaves.
- Dairy-free: Most commercial mayonnaise is dairy-free; check the label.
- Add 2 tbsp sweet pickle relish or diced pickles for extra tang and crunch.
- For a kick, add a pinch of cayenne pepper or smoked paprika to the mix.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Egg salad keeps in an airtight container in the refrigerator for 3–4 days.
- Assemble sandwiches just before serving to prevent soggy bread.
- Eggs can be boiled and peeled up to 3 days ahead; store peeled eggs in the fridge.
- Do not freeze egg salad — the mayonnaise separates and the texture suffers.
