# Creamy Vanilla-Cinnamon Rice Pudding

> Old-fashioned stovetop rice pudding — soft rice suspended in vanilla-scented custard — the warm, cheap, one-pot dessert to make on a cold afternoon.

## Ingredients
- 90 g (1/2 cup) short-grain white rice, rinsed until the water runs clear
- 960 ml (4 cups) whole milk
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 60 g (1/3 cup) granulated sugar
- 70 g (1/2 cup) raisins, optional
- 1 large egg yolk, lightly beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, plus more for serving
- 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter

## Instructions
1. Combine the rinsed rice, milk, and salt in a heavy medium saucepan and bring to a bare simmer over medium heat, stirring once or twice, 5–6 min.
2. Reduce the heat to low and cook uncovered, stirring every few minutes and scraping the bottom, until the rice is tender and the milk has thickened, 30–35 min.
3. Stir in the sugar and raisins and cook, stirring often, until the sugar dissolves and the pudding coats the back of a spoon, 8–10 min.
4. Ladle about 60 ml (1/4 cup) of the hot pudding into the beaten egg yolk while whisking, then scrape the mixture back into the pan.
5. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until the pudding thickens slightly and a bubble or two breaks at the edge, 2–3 min — do not let it boil.
6. Off the heat, stir in the vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and butter until the butter melts and the pudding is glossy.
7. Cool 10 min off the heat to thicken (it sets further as it stands), then spoon into bowls and dust with extra cinnamon.

## Notes & Substitutions
- **Vegan / dairy-free:** use 960 ml (4 cups) oat or coconut milk, swap the butter for coconut oil, and skip the egg yolk — whisk 1 tbsp cornstarch into the sugar before adding it in step 3 to keep the body.
- **Gluten-free:** naturally gluten-free as written; check that your raisins and vanilla are certified GF.
- **Lighter:** use 2% milk and cut the sugar to 40 g (3 tbsp); the pudding will be a little looser, so simmer 5 min longer in step 2.
- **Rice swaps:** arborio works as-is; brown rice needs about 20 min extra and 240 ml (1 cup) more milk.
- **No raisins?** Dried cranberries, chopped apricots, or a handful of toasted almonds stirred in at the end all work.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate in an airtight container with plastic pressed onto the surface for up to 4 days; the pudding firms up considerably when cold.
- Reheat gently on the stovetop or in 30-second microwave bursts, loosening with a splash of milk or water.
- Freezes for up to 2 months, though the rice softens and the texture turns porridge-like — thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat with extra milk.
- Make ahead: cook through step 6 up to 2 days in advance and refrigerate; add the final cinnamon dusting just before serving.
