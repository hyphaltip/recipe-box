# Eggs Benedict

> A classic brunch centerpiece: toasted English muffins topped with Canadian bacon, poached eggs, and silky lemon-butter hollandaise — perfect for weekend mornings or holiday gatherings.

## Ingredients
- 4 English muffins, split
- 8 slices Canadian bacon (or ham)
- 8 large eggs
- 2 tbsp white vinegar, for poaching water
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, finely chopped (optional, for garnish)

**For the hollandaise:**
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 113 g (1/2 cup / 1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions
1. Fill a large skillet or saucepan with 5 cm (2 inches) of water, add the vinegar, and bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
2. Melt the butter in a small saucepan over low heat or in the microwave; keep warm but not hot.
3. Whisk the egg yolks and lemon juice in a heatproof bowl set over a pot of barely simmering water (double boiler) until pale and thickened, 2–3 minutes.
4. Slowly drizzle in the warm melted butter while whisking constantly until the sauce is thick and glossy, 2–3 minutes; season with salt and cayenne, then remove from heat and keep warm.
5. Toast the English muffin halves in a toaster or under the broiler until golden, 3–4 minutes.
6. Warm the Canadian bacon in a large skillet over medium heat until lightly browned, 2–3 minutes per side.
7. Poach the eggs: crack each egg into a small bowl, then slide gently into the simmering water; cook until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, 3–4 minutes.
8. Remove the eggs with a slotted spoon and drain briefly on a paper towel-lined plate.
9. Assemble: place 2 muffin halves on each plate, top each with a slice of Canadian bacon, then a poached egg.
10. Spoon hollandaise generously over each egg, sprinkle with chives, and serve immediately.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a lighter version, swap Canadian bacon for smoked salmon or turkey bacon, and use a hollandaise made with half the butter plus a spoonful of Greek yogurt for creaminess.
- To make it gluten-free, use gluten-free English muffins.
- For a dairy-free hollandaise, substitute the butter with a high-quality plant-based butter; the sauce will still emulsify but may be slightly thinner.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Hollandaise is best made fresh and does not reheat well; if needed, keep it warm in a thermos or over warm water for up to 1 hour.
- Poached eggs can be made up to 2 days ahead: after poaching, transfer to a bowl of ice water and refrigerate; reheat in simmering water for 30–60 seconds before assembling.
- Assemble all components just before serving for the best texture and flavor.
