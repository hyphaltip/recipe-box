# French Toast

> Golden, custardy slices of bread with crisp edges—the ultimate weekend breakfast.

## Ingredients
- 8 slices thick-cut brioche or challah bread, about 1 inch thick
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided, for the pan
- Maple syrup, for serving

## Instructions
1. In a shallow dish, whisk together eggs, milk, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt until smooth and frothy.
2. Dip each bread slice into the egg mixture, soaking 15–20 seconds per side until fully saturated.
3. Melt 1/2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
4. Cook 2 soaked bread slices until golden brown and crisp, 2–3 minutes per side.
5. Transfer cooked slices to a plate and keep warm.
6. Repeat with remaining butter and bread, working in batches.
7. Serve warm with maple syrup.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a dairy-free version, use oat or almond milk and vegan butter.
- For gluten-free, use gluten-free bread (slightly stale works best).
- For a lighter version, use whole-grain bread and omit the sugar in the custard.
- Stale bread (1–2 days old) absorbs the custard better than fresh bread.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container up to 3 days; reheat in a toaster or skillet until crisp.
- Freeze cooked slices up to 2 months; reheat from frozen in a toaster.
- The custard can be whisked up to 24 hours ahead and refrigerated.
