# Greek Lemon Potatoes

> Golden, lemon-soaked roasted potatoes braised in olive oil and broth until creamy inside and crisp at the edges — the classic taverna side that goes with anything off the grill.

## Ingredients
- ½ cup (120 ml) chicken broth or water
- ½ cup (120 ml) olive oil
- ½ cup (120 ml) freshly squeezed lemon juice (from 3–4 large lemons)
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 3 lb (1.4 kg) large Yukon Gold potatoes (about 6), peeled, then halved lengthwise and crosswise
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano (optional)
- Flaky salt and black pepper, for serving

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C) with a rack in the middle.
2. Whisk the broth, olive oil, lemon juice, kosher salt, and oregano (if using) together in a 9x13-inch baking dish until the salt dissolves.
3. Add the potatoes and toss until every piece is coated, then arrange them cut-side down in a single layer.
4. Roast uncovered for 30 minutes, until the liquid is bubbling and the potatoes have started to take on color.
5. Remove the dish and baste the potatoes with the pan juices, turning them once; return to the oven.
6. Continue roasting until the potatoes are fork-tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed, 30–40 minutes more.
7. Raise the heat to 475°F (245°C) and roast until the edges are deep golden and crisp, 8–10 minutes, watching closely so the lemon juices don't scorch.
8. Rest 5 minutes, spoon the remaining pan juices over the top, and finish with flaky salt and black pepper.

## Notes & Substitutions
- **Vegan:** swap the chicken broth for vegetable broth or water — the olive oil and lemon carry all the flavor.
- **Lighter:** cut the olive oil to ⅓ cup and add 2 extra tablespoons of broth; the potatoes will be a little less silky but still lemony and tender.
- **Salt watch:** 1 tablespoon of kosher salt is right for Diamond Crystal; if using fine table salt, start with 2 teaspoons.
- **Variations:** tuck in 4 smashed garlic cloves, a pinch of red-pepper flakes, or 1 teaspoon smoked paprika before roasting; rosemary can stand in for the oregano.
- **Potato swap:** russets turn softer and more mashed-textured; baby gold potatoes work whole and cook about 10 minutes faster.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 4 days; the potatoes soak up the juices and get even more lemony.
- Reheat on a sheet pan at 425°F (220°C) for 10–12 minutes, or in a covered skillet over medium heat with a splash of water, until hot and re-crisped.
- Freeze for up to 2 months in a sealed container; thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat at high heat — texture softens slightly.
- Prep ahead: peel and cut the potatoes up to 1 day in advance and hold them in a bowl of cold water in the fridge; whisk the broth-oil-lemon mixture up to 2 days ahead and refrigerate, then re-whisk before using.
