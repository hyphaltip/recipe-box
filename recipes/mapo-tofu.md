# Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐)

> Sichuan's great cheap comfort food: soft tofu in a glossy, garlicky,
  chili-bean sauce with a little ground pork and a dusting of
  mouth-tingling Sichuan peppercorn. 25 minutes, deeply satisfying.

## Ingredients

- 400 g / 14 oz medium-firm or soft tofu, cut in 1.5 cm / 2/3 inch cubes
- 1 tsp salt, for the tofu water
- 100 g / 3.5 oz ground pork (or 100 g minced shiitake mushrooms)
- 1.5 tbsp doubanjiang (Sichuan chili bean paste)
- 1 tsp fermented black beans (douchi), rinsed and lightly smashed, optional
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, sliced
- 250 ml / 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water
- 1 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn, plus more to serve
- 1 tsp chili oil or flakes, optional, to taste
- steamed rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Simmer the tofu cubes in salted water 2 minutes (this seasons them
   and keeps them from falling apart); drain and set aside.
2. In a wok or skillet over medium-high heat, cook the pork, breaking it
   up, until it crisps at the edges, 3-4 minutes. Spoon off all but
   1 tbsp of the fat.
3. Add the doubanjiang and douchi; fry 30 seconds until the oil turns
   red and fragrant. Add the garlic, ginger, and scallion whites; stir
   30 seconds.
4. Pour in the broth and soy sauce; slide in the tofu. Simmer gently
   4-5 minutes, nudging (never stirring hard) so the tofu absorbs the
   sauce.
5. Drizzle in the cornstarch slurry in two passes, nudging until the
   sauce clings to the tofu like velvet.
6. Off the heat, scatter the scallion greens and Sichuan peppercorn.
   Serve over rice, extra peppercorn at the table.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Doubanjiang (Pixian, if you can find it) is non-negotiable for the
  real flavor — one jar lasts months and unlocks many Sichuan dishes.
- Vegetarian/vegan: mushroom mince + vegetable broth; the dish stays
  satisfying because the chili bean paste carries it.
- Soft tofu is traditional and melts in the mouth; medium-firm is easier
  for beginners. Pressing is unnecessary here.
- Gluten-free: doubanjiang is usually GF but check the label; use tamari.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently so the tofu stays intact.
- The pork-and-paste base can be made ahead and reheated with fresh tofu.
- Not a freezer dish — tofu goes spongy.
