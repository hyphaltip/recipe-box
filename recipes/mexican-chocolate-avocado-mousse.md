# Mexican Chocolate Avocado Mousse

> Silky dark-chocolate mousse with a whisper of cinnamon and cayenne — the
> classic Mexican chocolate profile, secretly built on avocado and ready in
> 10 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe avocados, about 450 g / 1 lb flesh
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/3 cup maple syrup, plus more to taste
- 1/4 cup coconut cream or oat milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/8 tsp cayenne, plus more to finish, optional
- 1/4 tsp fine salt
- 55 g / 2 oz dark chocolate, melted and cooled slightly, optional for extra richness
- fresh berries and flaky salt, to serve

## Instructions

1. Blend the avocado flesh, cocoa, maple syrup, coconut cream, vanilla,
   cinnamon, cayenne (if using), and salt until completely smooth —
   scrape down twice; no flecks of avocado should remain.
2. Add the melted chocolate (if using) and blend until glossy.
3. Taste and adjust: more maple for sweetness, more cayenne a pinch at a
   time — it should register as warmth, not burn.
4. Spoon into 6 small glasses or ramekins, smooth the tops, and chill at
   least 1 hour. Serve with berries and a pinch of flaky salt.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Ripe avocado is non-negotiable — underripe tastes grassy no matter how
  much chocolate you throw at it.
- For kids, skip the cayenne; the cinnamon alone is lovely.
- Coconut cream makes it fudgier; oat milk lighter.
- Sweetener range is wide: 1/3-1/2 cup maple, or pitted dates (soak and
  blend, 6-8) for a whole-food version.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Refrigerate up to 2 days with plastic wrap pressed onto the surface
  (prevents browning).
- Not a good freezer candidate — it firms to ice.
- Serve chilled; it softens and dulls at room temperature.
