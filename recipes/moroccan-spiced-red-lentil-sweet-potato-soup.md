# Moroccan-Spiced Red Lentil & Sweet Potato Soup

> A warmly spiced, tomato-based red lentil and sweet potato soup built on
> cumin, cinnamon, and ginger — budget-friendly, freezer-friendly, and on
> the table in under an hour.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne, or to taste
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 200 g / 1 cup dried red lentils, rinsed
- 500 g / 1 lb sweet potato, peeled and cut into 1.5 cm / 1/2 in cubes
- 400 g / 14 oz can diced tomatoes
- 1.2 L / 5 cups vegetable broth
- 2 tbsp lemon juice, plus more to taste
- salt and black pepper, to taste
- chopped cilantro or parsley, to garnish
- harissa, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat; cook the onion and carrot with a pinch of salt until softened, 6-7 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, smoked paprika, and cayenne; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1 minute more.
3. Add the lentils, sweet potato, diced tomatoes, and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, partially covered, until the lentils and sweet potato are very tender, 22-25 minutes.
4. Off the heat, stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and pepper. For a thicker, more rustic texture, mash some of the sweet potato against the side of the pot.
5. Ladle into bowls, top with cilantro or parsley, and pass harissa at the table for those who want more heat.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Stir in a handful of chopped dates or golden raisins with the tomatoes for a sweeter, more traditional Moroccan profile.
- No harissa on hand: a drizzle of chili oil or a pinch of extra cayenne gives a similar lift.
- For extra protein and texture, stir in a can of drained chickpeas along with the lentils.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Keeps 5 days refrigerated in an airtight container; the soup thickens considerably as it sits, so thin with broth or water when reheating.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months in individual portions for easy weekday lunches.
