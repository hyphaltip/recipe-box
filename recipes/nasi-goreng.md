# Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice)

> Indonesia's everyday fried rice, sweet-savory with kecap manis and punchy
> with sambal — the best possible fate for leftover rice, on the table in
> 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups day-old cooked jasmine rice, cold and clumped apart
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp shrimp paste (terasi) or fish sauce, or 2 tsp soy sauce for vegetarian
- 1 tsp sambal oelek, plus more to serve
- 1.5 tbsp kecap manis (Indonesian sweet soy sauce)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cups shredded green cabbage
- 1 carrot, diced small
- 100 g / 1 cup green beans, trimmed and halved
- 3 scallions, sliced
- cucumber slices and tomato wedges, to serve
- fried egg, one per person, for topping, optional

## Instructions

1. Prep everything first — fried rice waits for no one. Whisk the eggs,
   and break the cold rice apart with wet hands until no clumps remain.
2. Scramble the eggs in half the oil over medium-high until just set;
   scoop out and set aside.
3. Add the remaining oil; stir-fry the garlic and shallots 30 seconds,
   then the shrimp paste and sambal until fragrant, another 30 seconds.
4. Add the carrot and green beans; stir-fry until crisp-tender, 2 minutes,
   then the cabbage 1 minute more.
5. Add the rice and toss relentlessly, pressing and flipping, until every
   grain is separate and steaming, 3-4 minutes.
6. Return the eggs, add the kecap manis and soy sauce, and toss 1 minute
   until glossy and evenly coated. Finish with scallions. Serve with
   cucumber, tomato, sambal, and a fried egg on top.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Day-old cold rice is non-negotiable — fresh rice steams into mush. No
  leftovers? Spread hot rice on a tray and freeze 30 minutes.
- Add protein: diced chicken thigh or peeled shrimp stir-fried right after
  the aromatics (no longer vegetarian).
- Gluten-free: replace kecap manis with 1 tbsp tamari + 1 tsp palm sugar
  or brown sugar.
- The kecap manis makes it — find it in any Asian grocery.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Best fresh from the wok; keeps 2 days refrigerated, reheat piping hot.
- Cook rice in advance on purpose — refrigerate within an hour of cooking
  and use within 2 days (standard cooked-rice safety).
- Prep the aromatics and veg up to 2 days ahead.
