# No-Bake Chocolate Peanut Butter Oat Cookies

> A classic stovetop no-bake cookie with chocolate, peanut butter, and oats — ready in minutes without turning on the oven.

## Ingredients
- 2 cups (400 g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) milk
- 1/2 cup (113 g) margarine (1 cube/stick)
- 3 Tbsp (18 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 3 cups (240 g) rolled oats
- 1/2 cup (128 g) peanut butter
- 1 tsp (4 g) vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. In a large saucepan, combine sugar, milk, margarine, and cocoa powder.
2. Bring to a full rolling boil over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally; boil for 3 minutes.
3. Remove from heat.
4. Stir in oats, peanut butter, and vanilla until the mixture thickens to the right consistency, 1-2 minutes.
5. Drop by rounded teaspoons onto wax paper-lined baking sheets.
6. Let cool until set, 15-20 minutes.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a dairy-free version, use plant-based milk (oat or almond) and vegan margarine or coconut oil.
- For a gluten-free version, use certified gluten-free rolled oats.
- For a nut-free version, swap peanut butter for sunflower seed butter.
- For a lighter version, reduce sugar to 1 1/2 cups — cookies will be slightly less firm.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week, or refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.
- Freeze for up to 3 months; thaw at room temperature before serving.
- To soften, microwave for 5-10 seconds.
- These cookies can be made up to 3 days ahead — they hold their shape and flavor well.
