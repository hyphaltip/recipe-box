# No-Knead Whole Wheat Dutch Oven Bread

> A crackling crust and tender, nutty crumb from a dough that takes 5 minutes
> to mix and never needs kneading — time and a hot Dutch oven do all the work.

## Ingredients

- 400 g / 4 cups whole wheat flour
- 100 g / 3/4 cup bread flour or all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp fine salt
- 1/4 tsp instant yeast
- 420 g / 1 3/4 cups warm water
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup, optional (leaves it vegan if omitted)
- extra flour or cornmeal, for dusting

## Instructions

1. Stir the flours, salt, and yeast together in a large bowl. Add the water
   (and honey, if using) and stir until no dry flour remains — a wet, shaggy
   dough is right. Cover with a plate or plastic.
2. Let rise at room temperature 12-18 hours, until domed, bubbly, and
   stretchy. Overnight is perfect; a cool kitchen may need the full 18.
3. Flour your hands and fold the dough onto itself 4-5 times to make a
   round ball; let it rest seam-side down 15 minutes.
4. Shape: cup the dough and pull it toward you in circles to build surface
   tension. Place seam-side up in a floured towel-lined bowl, cover, and
   rise until puffy, 1-2 hours (or refrigerate overnight and bake cold).
5. About 30 minutes before baking, put the Dutch oven (lid on) in the oven
   and heat to 245 °C / 475 °F.
6. Carefully tip the dough into the screaming-hot pot (seam down — the
   rough seam becomes the rustic top), slash once with scissors or a knife,
   cover, and bake 30 minutes.
7. Uncover and bake 10-15 minutes more, until deeply browned and the loaf
   sounds hollow; internal temperature 96-99 °C / 205-210 °F. Cool at least
   45 minutes before slicing — the crust keeps crackling as it cools.

## Notes & Substitutions

- The Dutch oven traps steam from the dough, which is the entire secret of
  the crust. Enamel, cast iron, or ceramic all work (no plastic knobs).
- All whole wheat: skip the bread flour, add 20 g extra water, and expect a
  denser, earthier loaf.
- Whole wheat flour goes rancid — store it in the freezer and buy fresh.
- Cornbread-style mix-ins: 1 cup toasted sunflower seeds or walnuts at the
  fold stage.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Counter (cut side down on a board): 3 days. Freezer: 3 months, sliced.
- Revive day-old slices with 1 minute in a toaster.
- The overnight rise IS the make-ahead: mix after dinner, bake for breakfast.
