# Oatmeal Cookies

> Classic chewy oatmeal cookies made with pantry staples — perfect for lunchboxes or an afternoon treat.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (205 g) vegetable shortening (such as Crisco), at room temperature
- 1 cup (220 g) packed brown sugar
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups (135 g) rolled oats

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Grease baking sheets or line them with parchment paper.
3. In a large bowl, cream together the shortening, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy, 2-3 minutes.
4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla extract until combined.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
6. Gradually add the dry mixture to the wet mixture, stirring until just combined.
7. Fold in the rolled oats until evenly distributed throughout the dough.
8. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto the prepared baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches (5 cm) apart.
9. Bake until golden brown around the edges, 10-12 minutes.
10. Let cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a vegan version, replace the eggs with 2 flax eggs (2 tablespoons ground flaxseed whisked with 6 tablespoons water, rested 5 minutes); the cookies will be slightly denser but still delicious.
- Swap half the shortening for unsalted butter for a richer, more buttery flavor and a slightly crispier edge.
- Add 1/2 cup raisins, chocolate chips, or chopped nuts for extra texture and flavor.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Store baked cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
- Freeze baked cookies for up to 3 months; thaw at room temperature before serving.
- The dough can be refrigerated for up to 3 days, or frozen in scooped portions for up to 3 months. Bake frozen dough directly, adding 2-3 minutes to the bake time.
