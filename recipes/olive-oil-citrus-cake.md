# Olive Oil Citrus Cake

> A sunlit Mediterranean cake: olive oil and Greek yogurt keep it moist for
> days, while orange and lemon perfume every crumb. One bowl, one pan, no
> frosting required.

## Ingredients

- 240 g / 2 cups all-purpose flour (or half white whole wheat)
- 1.5 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 180 g / 3/4 cup sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 240 g / 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 120 ml / 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil (fruity, not peppery-heavy)
- zest of 2 oranges and 1 lemon
- 60 ml / 1/4 cup fresh orange juice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Glaze: 2 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp orange juice, warmed
- a few strips of orange zest and chopped pistachios, optional, to finish

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C / 350 °F. Grease a 23 cm / 9 inch round or
   springform pan and line the bottom with parchment.
2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
3. In a large bowl, whisk the sugar with the citrus zest until it smells
   like a citrus grove — this bruises the oils out of the zest. Whisk in
   the eggs, one at a time, until pale and slightly thickened.
4. Whisk in the yogurt, olive oil, orange juice, and vanilla, then fold in
   the dry ingredients just until smooth — don't overmix.
5. Scrape into the pan and bake 35-45 minutes, until deep gold and a
   skewer comes out clean. Cool 15 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.
6. While still warm, brush the top with the honey-orange glaze. Finish
   with pistachios and zest if you feel fancy. Serve warm or at room
   temperature.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Yogurt + olive oil replaces nearly all the butter of a classic cake —
  the crumb stays tender and actually improves overnight.
- A mild, fruity olive oil is the move; a harsh one will fight the citrus.
- Lemon-only version: swap the orange juice for lemon and add 1 tbsp more
  yogurt (lovely with blueberries folded in).
- Cake flour swap: 220 g cake flour for an even softer crumb.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Covered at room temperature 3 days — the glaze keeps the top moist.
- Freezes beautifully: 2 months, glazed but unadorned.
- Bake the day before serving; it slices cleaner on day two.
