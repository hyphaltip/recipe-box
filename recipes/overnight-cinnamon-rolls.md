# Overnight Cinnamon Rolls

> Soft, gooey, gently sweet rolls with a honey-yogurt cream cheese glaze —
> mixed tonight, baked straight from the fridge tomorrow morning, warm
> rolls with no 5 a.m. effort.

## Ingredients

- 480 g / 4 cups all-purpose flour (or swap 160 g for white whole wheat)
- 50 g / 1/4 cup sugar
- 2.25 tsp instant yeast
- 1 tsp salt
- 240 ml / 1 cup warm whole milk
- 2 large eggs
- 60 g / 4 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- Filling: 45 g / 3 tbsp unsalted butter, very soft
- 90 g / 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tbsp ground cinnamon
- Glaze: 115 g / 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- 60 g / 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, sugar, yeast, and salt in a stand bowl. Add the milk,
   eggs, and butter; knead with the hook (or by hand) 8-10 minutes, until
   smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky.
2. Cover and rise until doubled, 1-1.5 hours.
3. Roll the dough into a 30 x 40 cm / 12 x 16 inch rectangle. Spread with
   the soft butter, then scatter the brown sugar and cinnamon evenly.
4. Roll tightly from the long side into a log; cut into 12 rolls with
   floss or a sharp knife (12 rolls fit a 23 x 33 cm / 9 x 13 inch pan).
5. Arrange in a buttered pan, cover, and refrigerate overnight (8-16
   hours) — the slow cold rise builds flavor. (Room-temp alternative:
   rise 1 hour and bake same day.)
6. Morning: take the pan out while the oven heats to 175 °C / 350 °F.
   Bake 22-26 minutes, until golden at the edges and barely set in the
   middle — an instant-read thermometer reads 88-90 °C / 190-195 °F.
7. Cool 10 minutes. Whisk the glaze ingredients until smooth and spread
   over the warm rolls. Pull apart and accept the compliments.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Unbaked rolls freeze beautifully: after shaping, freeze solid on a
  tray, then bag. Thaw overnight in the fridge and bake as normal.
- The glaze is deliberately lighter than buttercream — yogurt and honey
  instead of powdered sugar avalanche.
- Swap the filling: 3/4 cup finely chopped toasted pecans, or 1/2 cup
  raisins softened in hot water, cardamom for cinnamon.
- Whole milk + butter give the classic soft crumb; 2% milk works fine.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Best the day they're baked; keep 2 days airtight, reheat 10 seconds in
  the microwave to re-soften the glaze.
- The overnight fridge rise is the make-ahead — bake fresh any morning.
- Baked (unglazed) rolls freeze 2 months; glaze after reheating.
