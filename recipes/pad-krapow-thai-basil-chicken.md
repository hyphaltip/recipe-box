# Pad Krapow (Thai Basil Chicken)

> A 25-minute Thai street-food stir-fry of garlicky ground chicken, chilies,
> and a pile of fresh basil over rice — with a crispy fried egg on top.

## Ingredients

- 500 g / 1 lb ground chicken (or pork)
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2-4 Thai chilies, or 1/2-1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 cups fresh Thai basil (or sweet basil), leaves only
- 1.5 cups jasmine rice, cooked, for serving
- 4 large eggs, for frying
- Sauce:
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
  - 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or vegetarian "mushroom" stir-fry sauce)
  - 1 tsp dark soy sauce or 1/2 tsp sugar
  - 2 tsp water

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice. Meanwhile, stir the sauce ingredients together and set
   aside near the stove.
2. Finely chop the garlic and chilies together into a rough paste (a mortar
   is traditional; a knife works fine).
3. Heat the oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat until shimmering;
   add the garlic-chili paste and stir-fry 20 seconds, until fragrant but
   not browned.
4. Add the chicken, pressing it flat then breaking it up; stir-fry until no
   longer pink and browning in spots, 4-5 minutes.
5. Pour in the sauce and toss 30 seconds; kill the heat, add the basil, and
   stir until just wilted, 15-20 seconds.
6. Fry the eggs in a lightly oiled pan over medium-high until the edges are
   lacy-crisp and the yolk still runs. Serve the chicken over rice, egg on
   top, breaking the yolk over everything.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Heat control: start with 1 chili and add more — the dish should be spicy
  but still balanced by the salty-sweet sauce.
- Vegetarian: ground firm tofu (press and crumble) or chopped mushrooms
  with vegetarian stir-fry sauce instead of oyster sauce.
- Regular basil works in a pinch; add a pinch of ground anise for the
  licorice note of Thai basil.
- Gluten-free: use tamari and a GF oyster sauce alternative.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Best eaten fresh — the basil dulls on storage.
- The cooked chicken (without basil) keeps 2 days; reheat hot, then stir in
  fresh basil.
- Double the sauce and keep it in the fridge for a week of fast stir-fries.
