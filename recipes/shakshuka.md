# Shakshuka (Eggs Poached in Spiced Tomato Sauce)

> A North African and Middle Eastern classic: eggs gently poached in a smoky,
> spiced tomato and pepper sauce — one pan, any time of day.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced thin
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced thin
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes, or to taste
- 800 g / 28 oz canned crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 tsp sugar, optional
- 4-6 large eggs
- 60 g / 1/2 cup crumbled feta, optional
- small handful parsley or cilantro, chopped
- salt and black pepper, to taste
- warm crusty bread or pita, to serve

## Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat; cook the onion and
   bell pepper with a pinch of salt until soft and lightly browned, 10-12 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and spices; stir until fragrant, about 1 minute.
3. Pour in the tomatoes, add the sugar if using, and simmer until thickened
   enough to hold a spoon-trail, 8-10 minutes; season with salt and pepper.
4. Make small wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon and crack an egg
   into each; season the eggs lightly.
5. Cover and cook 5-7 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks still
   run (or to your liking).
6. Scatter with feta and herbs; serve straight from the pan with bread.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Dairy-free: skip the feta — a drizzle of tahini adds creaminess instead.
- Add greens: stir in a few handfuls of spinach with the tomatoes.
- Heartier version: crumble in spicy merguez or chickpeas (keeps it
  vegetarian) with the tomatoes.
- Canned whole tomatoes, hand-crushed, are a fine swap for crushed.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- The sauce keeps 4 days refrigerated and freezes well (up to 3 months) —
  reheat, then poach fresh eggs in it.
- Cooked shakshuka with eggs is best eaten the same day; yolks firm up on
  reheating.
