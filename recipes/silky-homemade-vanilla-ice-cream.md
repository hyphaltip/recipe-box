# Silky Homemade Vanilla Ice Cream

> A silky, four-ingredient vanilla ice cream that's perfect for summer afternoons, warm pie, or a simple scoop on its own.

## Ingredients
- 540 ml (2 ¼ cups) whole milk
- 240 ml (1 cup) heavy cream
- 150 g (¾ cup) white sugar
- 10 ml (2 teaspoons) pure vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Stir the milk, cream, and sugar together in a saucepan over low heat until the sugar has dissolved and the mixture is just hot, with a small ring of foam around the edge, 4-5 minutes.
2. Transfer the hot cream mixture to a pourable container, such as a large measuring cup.
3. Stir in the vanilla extract.
4. Chill the cream mixture thoroughly, 2 hours to overnight.
5. Pour the cold cream mixture into an ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions, 20 to 25 minutes, until thick and softly frozen.
6. Serve immediately, or press a piece of plastic wrap directly onto the surface and freeze to firm and ripen, 2 to 3 hours.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a lighter version, use 2% milk and half-and-half instead of whole milk and heavy cream; the texture will be slightly less rich but still creamy.
- For a dairy-free/vegan version, substitute full-fat coconut milk for the milk and coconut cream for the heavy cream — expect a coconut-forward flavor.
- This recipe is naturally gluten-free and nut-free.
- For a deeper vanilla flavor, split and scrape one vanilla bean into the warm mixture along with the extract.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- The base can be made and chilled up to 24 hours ahead before churning.
- Churned ice cream keeps in the freezer for up to 2 weeks; press plastic wrap directly onto the surface and cover tightly to prevent ice crystals.
- Let the ice cream sit at room temperature for 5-10 minutes before scooping for easier serving.
