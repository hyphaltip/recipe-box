# Simple Lentil Soup

> A humble, nourishing lentil soup made from just two pantry staples — perfect for a cozy weeknight dinner.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200 g) dried green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 2 cups (500 ml) water
- 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the lentils under cold running water until the water runs clear.
2. Combine the lentils, water, and salt in a medium saucepan.
3. Bring to a boil over high heat.
4. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer until the lentils are tender, 25-30 minutes.
5. Season with black pepper to taste and serve warm.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Swap the water for vegetable broth to add depth and savoriness.
- For a thinner, more brothy soup, increase the water to 4 cups (1 L).
- For a heartier version, sauté 1 diced onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, and 2 diced carrots in 1 tbsp olive oil before adding the lentils.
- Naturally vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
- Freeze for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator.
- Reheat gently on the stovetop over medium-low heat, adding a splash of water or broth if the soup has thickened.
- The soup thickens as it sits; stir in extra liquid when reheating.
