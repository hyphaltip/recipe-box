# Tartine Bread (Basic Country Dough)

> A naturally leavened, 75% hydration country loaf with a crackling crust and open, custardy crumb — the signature bread from San Francisco's Tartine Bakery, ideal for weekend baking.

## Ingredients
- 700 g water, at 80°F (27°C) (about 3 cups)
- 50 g water, at 80°F (27°C), for dissolving the salt (about 3 ½ tablespoons)
- 200 g leaven, active and bubbly, 100% hydration (about ¾ cup + 2 tablespoons)
- 900 g white bread flour (about 7 ½ cups)
- 100 g whole wheat flour (about ¾ cup + 1 tablespoon)
- 20 g fine sea salt (about 1 tablespoon + ¼ teaspoon)

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine 700 g water (80°F) and 200 g leaven; stir until the leaven is fully dispersed, 1-2 min.
2. Add 900 g white flour and 100 g whole wheat flour; mix with your hands until no dry flour remains, 2-3 min.
3. Cover and rest (autolyse) 25-40 min.
4. Dissolve 20 g salt in the remaining 50 g water; pour over the dough and fold until fully incorporated, 3-5 min.
5. Cover and bulk ferment at 75-80°F (24-27°C) for 3-4 hours, performing 4 sets of folds at 30-min intervals — each set: fold the dough over itself from all four sides until it tightens, about 30 sec per set.
6. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface; divide into 2 equal pieces (about 985 g each), 2-3 min.
7. Shape each piece into a tight boule, 5-10 min.
8. Place the boules seam-side up in floured proofing baskets; cover and proof at room temperature 2-3 hours, or refrigerate 8-12 hours for a more developed flavor.
9. About 45 min before baking, preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C) with a Dutch oven inside.
10. Turn one boule onto parchment paper, seam-side down; score the top with a lame or sharp knife, 1-2 sec.
11. Transfer to the hot Dutch oven, cover, and bake 20 min.
12. Uncover and bake until deep golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 205-210°F (96-99°C), 20-25 min more.
13. Cool on a wire rack at least 1 hour before slicing.

## Notes & Substitutions
- This bread is naturally vegan, dairy-free, and nut-free.
- For a heartier, higher-fiber loaf, increase whole wheat flour to 200 g and reduce white flour to 800 g; expect a slightly denser crumb.
- To make it gluten-free, this recipe won't work as written; use a gluten-free flour blend with xanthan gum and expect a much wetter dough.
- If you don't have an active leaven, build one from a mature starter: feed it 100 g flour + 100 g water 8-12 hours before mixing.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Baked loaves keep at room temperature, wrapped in a cloth or paper bag, for 2-3 days; slice and freeze for up to 3 months.
- To refresh a stale loaf, warm it in a 350°F (175°C) oven for 5-10 min.
- Make-ahead: after shaping, refrigerate the proofing baskets overnight (8-12 hours) and bake directly from the fridge the next morning.
