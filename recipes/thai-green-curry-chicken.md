# Thai Green Curry Chicken

> A 30-minute one-pan curry built on store-bought green curry paste
> bloomed in coconut cream — fragrant, gently fiery, and loaded with
> chicken and crisp vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp Thai green curry paste (Mae Ploy or Aroy-D are reliable)
- 400 ml / 1 can full-fat coconut milk, undisturbed (or 1/2 cup thick
  coconut cream scooped from the top)
- 450 g / 1 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut in bite-sized pieces
- 150 g / 2 cups green beans, trimmed
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 small zucchini or Thai eggplant, halved and sliced
- 1.5 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- 1 cup Thai basil leaves (or sweet basil)
- 1 lime, cut in wedges
- steamed jasmine rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Open the coconut milk without shaking; spoon the thick cream into a
   wok or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the curry paste and fry,
   stirring, 2-3 minutes until deeply fragrant and slightly darkened —
   this bloom is the flavor foundation.
2. Add the chicken and toss to coat in the paste for 1-2 minutes.
3. Pour in the remaining coconut milk; simmer until the chicken is
   cooked through, 6-8 minutes.
4. Add the green beans, bell pepper, and zucchini; simmer until
   crisp-tender, 4-5 minutes.
5. Season with fish sauce and sugar; taste — it should be salty-spicy
   with a hint of sweet. Off the heat, stir in the basil until wilted.
   Serve over rice with lime wedges.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Frying the paste in coconut cream (instead of adding it all at once)
  is the difference between "green curry" and "green soup."
- Spice control is in the paste brand and quantity: start with 2 tbsp.
  Extra heat at the table: sliced chilies in fish sauce.
- Vegetarian: firm tofu + soy sauce for the fish sauce; check the paste
  label (some contain shrimp paste — vegetarian pastes exist).
- Shrimp version: add peeled shrimp in the last 3 minutes.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently, add fresh basil after.
- Freezes 2 months (texture of the veg softens slightly).
- The bloomed paste + chicken base can be made ahead; add veg fresh.
