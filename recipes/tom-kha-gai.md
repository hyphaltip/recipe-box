# Tom Kha Gai (Thai Coconut Chicken Soup)

> Thailand's creamy-sour coconut chicken soup — silky broth brightened
> with lime and sharpened with galangal, lemongrass, and fish sauce. A
> 30-minute pot that tastes like it simmered all day.

## Ingredients

- 450 g / 1 lb boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 400 ml / 1 can coconut milk (full-fat)
- 500 ml / 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 lemongrass stalks, tough outer layers removed, bruised and tied in knots
- 5 cm / 2 inch piece galangal (or ginger), cut in thin coins
- 6 makrut (kaffir) lime leaves, torn — or the zest of 1 lime
- 225 g / 8 oz mushrooms (cremini, oyster, or shiitake), sliced
- 2.5 tbsp fish sauce
- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 2 tsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- 1-2 bird's eye chilies, thinly sliced, optional
- a handful of cilantro and sliced scallions, to serve

## Instructions

1. In a pot, bring the broth, coconut milk, lemongrass, galangal, and
   lime leaves to a bare simmer. Steep 10 minutes to infuse — do not boil
   hard, or the coconut milk can split.
2. Add the chicken slices and simmer gently until just cooked through,
   6-8 minutes.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook 2-3 minutes.
4. Off the heat, stir in the fish sauce, lime juice, and sugar. Now taste
   and balance: it should be sour first, then salty, then sweet, with
   coconut richness underneath. Fish sauce for salty, lime for sour,
   sugar rounds.
5. Fish out the lemongrass knots (and lime leaves if you like). Serve
   with chilies, cilantro, and scallions over steamed jasmine rice if
   you want to make it a full meal.

## Notes & Substitutions

- The aromatics are for perfume, not eating — diners push them aside,
  Thai-style.
- Galangal is worth finding (frozen works); ginger is the acceptable
  stand-in with a different, sharper warmth.
- Vegetarian: vegetable broth + soy sauce for the fish sauce, extra
  mushrooms, cubed firm tofu instead of chicken.
- Lighter: light coconut milk saves ~60 kcal per bowl; the soup keeps
  most of its body.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Best fresh; keeps 2 days refrigerated, reheat to a bare simmer.
- The infused base (step 1) can be made a day ahead.
- Don't freeze — coconut milk soups go grainy.
