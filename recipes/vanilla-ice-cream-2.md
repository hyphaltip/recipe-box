# Vanilla Ice Cream

> A classic, five-ingredient vanilla ice cream that's creamy, rich, and perfect for summer — or any time you crave a homemade frozen treat.

## Ingredients
- 2 ¼ cups (540 ml) whole milk
- 1 cup (240 ml) heavy cream
- ¾ cup (150 g) white sugar
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Stir milk, cream, and sugar together in a saucepan over low heat until sugar has dissolved and a small ring of foam appears around the edge, 4-5 minutes.
2. Transfer the hot cream mixture to a pourable container, such as a large measuring cup, and stir in vanilla extract.
3. Chill the mixture thoroughly in the refrigerator, at least 2 hours or up to overnight.
4. Pour the cold mixture into an ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions, 20-25 minutes.
5. Serve immediately if you prefer soft-serve, or transfer to a freezer-safe container, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface, and freeze until firm, 2-3 hours.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a dairy-free version, substitute full-fat coconut milk for the whole milk and coconut cream for the heavy cream.
- For a lighter version, use whole milk and half-and-half instead of heavy cream — the texture will be less rich but still creamy.
- Use a high-quality pure vanilla extract, or scrape one vanilla bean into the warm mixture, for the best flavor.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Store in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.
- The cream mixture can be made up to 24 hours ahead and chilled before churning.
- To soften frozen ice cream, let it sit at room temperature for 5-10 minutes before scooping.
