# Vanilla Ice Cream

> A classic custard-based vanilla ice cream, perfect for summer desserts.

## Ingredients
- 480 ml (2 cups) heavy cream
- 240 ml (1 cup) whole milk
- 150 g (3/4 cup) granulated sugar
- 5 large egg yolks
- 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped (or 2 teaspoons vanilla extract)
- 1/4 teaspoon fine salt

## Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, combine cream, milk, sugar, and salt over medium heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until steaming and sugar is dissolved, 4-5 minutes.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks. Slowly pour about 1 cup of the hot cream mixture into the yolks while whisking constantly to temper.
3. Pour the tempered yolk mixture back into the saucepan. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon, until the custard thickens enough to coat the back of the spoon, 5-7 minutes.
4. Remove from heat and strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a bowl. Stir in vanilla bean seeds (or vanilla extract).
5. Place the bowl over an ice bath and stir until cooled. Cover and refrigerate until completely chilled, at least 4 hours or overnight.
6. Pour the chilled custard into an ice cream maker and freeze according to manufacturer's instructions, 20 to 25 minutes.
7. Serve ice cream immediately when softly frozen or place a piece of plastic wrap directly onto ice cream and place in the freezer to firm and ripen, 2 to 3 hours.

## Notes & Substitutions
- For a dairy-free version, substitute full-fat coconut milk for the heavy cream and unsweetened almond milk for the whole milk; use coconut cream for extra richness.
- For a lighter version, use half-and-half instead of heavy cream — the texture will be less creamy but still delicious.
- Vanilla bean can be replaced with 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract, stirred in after straining.

## Storage & Make-Ahead
- Store in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.
- Let sit at room temperature for 5-10 minutes before serving after freezing to soften slightly.
- The custard base can be made up to 2 days ahead and refrigerated before churning.
