# West African Peanut Soup

> The beloved groundnut soup of West Africa: sweet potato and greens in a
> silky, gently spiced peanut broth — rich-tasting yet entirely plant-based.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut oil or neutral oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes, or to taste
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 170 g / 3/4 cup natural unsweetened peanut butter
- 1.5 liters / 6 cups vegetable broth
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, about 500 g, cubed
- 200 g / 7 oz collard greens or kale, stemmed and chopped
- 400 g / 14 oz can chickpeas, drained and rinsed, optional for protein
- 1 tbsp lime juice, plus wedges to serve
- 1.5 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- roasted peanuts and cilantro, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium; soften the onion and carrots
   with a pinch of salt, 6-7 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, and chili; stir 1 minute, then the tomato
   paste; cook until brick-red, 1-2 minutes.
3. Whisk the peanut butter into the broth until smooth (a fork and a bowl
   first makes this easier), then pour into the pot.
4. Add the sweet potatoes (and chickpeas, if using); simmer until the
   potatoes are tender, 18-20 minutes.
5. Stir in the greens and cook until silky, 4-5 minutes. Finish with lime
   juice and salt to taste — it should be savory, nutty, and a little
   bright. Serve with roasted peanuts, cilantro, and lime wedges.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Use natural peanut butter with one ingredient: peanuts. Sweetened PB
  makes the soup taste like a peanut butter cup.
- Nut-free: sunflower seed butter works surprisingly well.
- Not-vegan upgrades: stir in shredded cooked chicken at the end, or top
  with hard-boiled egg halves.
- Thin with broth to serve as a soup; leave thick and serve over rice as
  a stew.

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Refrigerate up to 5 days; freezes up to 3 months.
- Peanut solids may separate when stored — stir well while reheating.
- Freeze in individual portions for instant work lunches.
