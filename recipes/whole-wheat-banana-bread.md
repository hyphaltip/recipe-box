# Whole Wheat Banana Bread

> The freckled bananas on your counter, upgraded: maple syrup instead of a
> sugar avalanche, white whole wheat for a nutty crumb, Greek yogurt for
> keeping power, and toasted walnuts for crunch.

## Ingredients

- 3 large very ripe bananas (about 360 g / 1.5 cups mashed)
- 80 ml / 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 2 large eggs
- 60 g / 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 60 g / 1/4 cup melted coconut oil or unsalted butter
- 1.5 tsp vanilla extract
- 190 g / 1.5 cups white whole wheat flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 80 g / 3/4 cup walnuts, toasted and roughly chopped
- 60 g / 1/3 cup dark chocolate chips, optional
- 1 banana, split lengthwise, for a bakery-style top, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C / 350 °F. Butter a 23 x 13 cm / 9 x 5 inch
   loaf pan and line with parchment, leaving an overhang.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl (a few lumps are welcome). Whisk in
   the maple syrup, eggs, yogurt, melted coconut oil, and vanilla until
   smooth.
3. Sprinkle the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt over the top and
   fold just until no dry streaks remain. Fold in the walnuts (and
   chocolate, if using).
4. Scrape into the pan; lay the split banana on top if you want the
   caramelized bakery look. Bake 55-65 minutes, until deeply golden and a
   skewer comes out with only crumbs.
5. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then lift out by the parchment and cool on
   a rack. The texture is best fully cooled — and even better tomorrow.

## Notes & Substitutions

- Truly spotty bananas are the whole point: the riper they are, the less
  sweetener you need. Freeze surplus ripe bananas, thaw to use.
- Maple + banana is the flavor pairing doing the sweetening — resist
  adding sugar; taste the batter and trust it.
- White whole wheat is 100% whole grain but bakes like all-purpose;
  regular whole wheat works with a slightly denser result.
- Peanut butter swirl: drop 3 tbsp peanut butter on the batter and drag a
  knife through before baking.
- Egg swap: 1 extra banana + 1 tbsp yogurt (vegan then, if oil is used).

## Storage & Make-Ahead

- Counter 3 days; fridge 5 days (toast slices to revive).
- Freezer MVP: slice first, freeze solid, bag up to 3 months; toast
  straight from frozen.
- Bake two loaves while the oven is on — one for now, one for the freezer.
