Caribbean Curried Coconut Lentil & Sweet Potato Soup

A creamy, gently spiced red lentil and sweet potato soup simmered with curry powder, ginger, and coconut milk, brightened with lime — a one-pot dinner that's ready in under an hour.

50 min total (15 prep + 35 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyCaribbean · dinner

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp coconut oil or neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1/2 to 1 Scotch bonnet or habanero pepper, seeded and minced, or to taste (optional)
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 200 g / 1 cup dried red lentils, rinsed
  • 500 g / 1 lb sweet potato, peeled and cut into 1.5 cm / 1/2 in cubes
  • 1 L / 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 400 ml / 14 oz can full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp lime juice, plus more to taste
  • salt, to taste
  • chopped cilantro or scallion, to garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat; cook the onion until softened, 5 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, and chile (if using); cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Stir in the curry powder, allspice, and thyme and toast for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the lentils, sweet potato, and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, partially covered, until the lentils and sweet potato are very tender, 20-25 minutes.
  4. Stir in the coconut milk and simmer 5 more minutes. For a creamier texture, blend one-third of the soup with an immersion blender, or mash some of the sweet potato against the side of the pot.
  5. Off the heat, stir in the lime juice and season with salt. Serve topped with cilantro or scallion.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Leave out the chile entirely, or serve hot sauce on the side, for a milder version that's still kid-friendly.
  • Swap the full-fat coconut milk for light coconut milk to cut richness; the soup will be a little thinner.
  • No Scotch bonnet on hand: a pinch of cayenne stands in fine, or skip the heat altogether.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Keeps 4-5 days refrigerated in an airtight container; the lentils continue to thicken the soup, so thin with a little broth or water when reheating.
  • Freezes well for up to 3 months; stir in fresh lime juice after reheating for the brightest flavor.
