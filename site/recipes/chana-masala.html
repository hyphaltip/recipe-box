Chana Masala (Spiced Chickpea Curry)

A restaurant-style chickpea curry built on properly browned onions and bloomed spices — rich, warming, vegan, and even better on day two.

50 min total (15 prep + 35 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyIndian · dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp coconut oil or neutral oil
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger, about a 5 cm / 2 inch piece
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp ground coriander
  • 1.5 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2-1 tsp cayenne, to taste
  • 800 g / 28 oz canned crushed tomatoes
  • 3 x 400 g / 14 oz cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1.5 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 tsp garam masala, plus more to finish
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, plus wedges to serve
  • a handful of cilantro, chopped
  • cooked basmati rice and/or naan, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pot over medium; cook the onions with a pinch of salt until deeply golden brown, 10-12 minutes — this is the flavor base, don't rush it.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute. Add the cumin, coriander, turmeric, and cayenne; stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes and sugar; simmer until the oil separates and darkens at the edges, 8-10 minutes.
  4. Add the chickpeas and salt; simmer uncovered 15 minutes, mashing a spoonful of chickpeas against the pot to thicken the gravy.
  5. Off the heat, stir in the garam masala, lemon juice, and cilantro. Season to taste and serve with rice and lemon wedges.

Notes & Substitutions

  • For restaurant-style tang, stir in 1 tsp amchur (dried mango powder) with the garam masala, or an extra squeeze of lemon.
  • Wilt in a few handfuls of spinach at the end for extra greens.
  • Lighter swap: 1 tbsp oil is enough if you use a nonstick pot — the onion browning needs patience more than fat.
  • Make it a full protein bowl: top with a poached egg or a dollop of yogurt (no longer vegan, still excellent).

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Refrigerate up to 5 days; freezes up to 3 months.
  • The flavor genuinely deepens overnight — a flagship batch-cook recipe.
  • Reheat gently; loosen with a splash of water if it thickens.
