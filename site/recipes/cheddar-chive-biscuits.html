Cheddar-Chive Whole Wheat Biscuits

Flaky layered biscuits in 25 minutes flat, whole wheat for real flavor, and enough sharp cheddar that you can cut the butter back to three tablespoons without anyone noticing. The trick is three quick folds — 90 seconds of work for tall, pull-apart layers.

25 min total (10 prep + 15 cook)8 servingseasy difficultyAmerican · quickbread

Ingredients

  • 200 g / 1.6 cups white whole wheat flour
  • 120 g / 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 45 g / 3 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 120 g / 1.3 cups shredded sharp cheddar, plus a handful for topping
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh chives
  • 240 ml / 1 cup cold buttermilk, plus 2 tbsp for brushing
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • pinch of paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 220°C / 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment. Whisk the flours, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Cut in the butter with your fingertips or a pastry blender until the largest pieces are pea-sized — a few flat flakes are good, they become layers. Stir in the cheddar and chives.
  3. Make a well, add the buttermilk and honey, and fold with a spatula just until a shaggy, slightly sticky dough forms. If dry flour remains in the bowl, add buttermilk a tablespoon at a time.
  4. Turn onto a floured counter and pat into a rough 20x15 cm rectangle, about 2 cm thick. Fold in thirds like a letter, rotate 90°, pat out again, and fold in thirds once more. Pat to 2.5 cm thick — this is the whole lamination; no rolling pin needed.
  5. Cut into 8 squares with a floured knife (no scraps to re-roll, so no tough biscuits) and space them apart on the sheet. Brush the tops with the extra buttermilk, add a pinch of cheddar and paprika to each.
  6. Bake until tall and deeply golden, 12–15 minutes. Cool 5 minutes — the cheese inside is lava.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Sharp or extra-sharp cheddar does the most work per gram; mild disappears into the dough.
  • Swap the chives for green onion tops, or the cheddar for pepper jack and a pinch of cayenne.
  • Squares beat rounds for a reason: no re-rolled scraps, and re-rolled dough bakes tough and flat.
  • No buttermilk: stir 1 tbsp lemon juice into 240 ml / 1 cup whole milk, rest 10 minutes.
  • The honey is subtle — it's there for browning and to round the whole wheat's slight bitterness, not sweetness.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Biscuits are best within hours of baking; split and toast day-two ones.
  • Freeze baked biscuits up to 2 months; reheat from frozen in a 180°C / 350°F oven for 8–10 minutes.
  • Cut biscuits freeze raw too: freeze on the sheet, then bag; bake from frozen, adding 2–3 minutes.
