Filipino Chicken Adobo

The national dish of the Philippines: chicken braised in soy, vinegar, garlic, and bay until the sauce reduces to a glossy, lip-smacking glaze. Six pantry ingredients, one pot, and it tastes even better tomorrow.

55 min total (10 prep + 45 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyFilipino · dinner

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg / 2.5 lb bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks, or 900 g boneless thighs
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup cane vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns, lightly cracked
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp sugar, optional to round the sauce
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • steamed rice, to serve

Instructions

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, peppercorns, bay leaves, sugar, and water in a wide pot or deep skillet. Bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce to a steady simmer, cover, and cook, turning the pieces occasionally, until tender, 25-30 minutes.
  3. Fish out the chicken. Uncover and boil the sauce hard until it reduces to a glossy, spoon-coating glaze, 8-12 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a separate pan and sear the chicken pieces, skin side down, until deeply browned and crisp, 4-5 minutes (optional but transformative).
  5. Return the chicken to the glaze and turn to coat. Serve over rice with plenty of the sauce.

Notes & Substitutions

  • The old rule: don't stir the vinegar early — let its raw edge cook off in the first 10 minutes of simmering, then taste and adjust.
  • Pork adobo (adobong baboy): swap in 900 g pork shoulder, simmer 40 minutes.
  • Gluten-free: use tamari — everything else is naturally GF.
  • Add 2 cubed potatoes in the last 20 minutes to soak up sauce (carbs up, joy up).
  • Turbo shortcut: skip the sear; the braise alone is already dinner.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Adobo is a famous keeper: refrigerate up to 5 days, freeze up to 3 months.
  • Flavor peaks on day two — a flagship batch-cook recipe.
  • Reheat gently; the glaze thickens when cold, so loosen with a splash of water.
