Classic Egg Salad Sandwich

A creamy, tangy lunchbox classic that comes together in under 30 minutes.

27 min total (15 prep + 12 cook)4 servingseasy difficultyAmerican · lunch

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 cup celery, finely diced
  • 2 tablespoons red onion, finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh chives, chopped
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 8 slices sandwich bread (whole wheat or white)

Instructions

  1. Place eggs in a single layer in a saucepan, cover with cold water by 1 inch, and bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Remove from heat, cover, and let stand for 10–12 minutes until fully set.
  3. Drain eggs and transfer to a bowl of ice water; cool for 5 minutes, then peel and chop into small pieces.
  4. In a large bowl, combine chopped eggs, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, celery, red onion, chives, salt, and pepper, stirring until well combined.
  5. Spread the egg salad evenly on 4 slices of bread, top with the remaining slices, and serve immediately.

Notes & Substitutions

  • For a lighter version, replace half or all of the mayonnaise with plain Greek yogurt.
  • For a dairy-free option, use vegan mayonnaise.
  • For a gluten-free option, serve on gluten-free bread or scoop into lettuce wraps.
  • Add a pinch of paprika or a dash of hot sauce for extra flavor.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Store egg salad in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
  • Make the egg salad up to 2 days ahead; add the bread just before serving to prevent sogginess.
  • Do not freeze egg salad, as the texture will become watery.
