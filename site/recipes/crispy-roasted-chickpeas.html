Crispy Roasted Chickpeas

Shatteringly crisp, spiced snack chickpeas from one sheet pan — the crunchy, fiber-packed alternative to chips.

40 min total (5 prep + 35 cook)4 servingseasy difficultyMediterranean · snack

Ingredients

  • 2 x 400 g / 14 oz cans chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and dried very well
  • 1.5 tbsp olive oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • Spice options (pick one):
  • Smoky: 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • Za'atar: 2 tsp za'atar
  • Curry: 1 tsp curry powder + 1/4 tsp cayenne

Nutrition per serving (~ est.)

~230Calories

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C / 400 °F. Spread the chickpeas on a clean towel and pat completely dry — any moisture steams them soft. Loose skins can be rubbed off and discarded (or left on; they crisp too).
  2. Toss the chickpeas on a rimmed sheet pan with the oil and salt; roast 30-40 minutes, shaking the pan every 10 minutes, until golden and rattle-tight when you shake the pan.
  3. Toss hot with your spice mix — the residual oil helps it stick — and cool 5 minutes on the pan; they crisp further as they cool.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Air fryer: 200 °C / 400 °F for 15-20 minutes, shaking twice.
  • Season after roasting, not before — spices burn at this temperature (salt with the oil is fine).
  • If they soften in storage, re-crisp 5 minutes in a hot oven.
  • Kid-friendly: skip the spice, toss with a little parmesan after roasting (no longer vegan).

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Airtight at room temperature up to 3 days (they're crispest day one).
  • Do not refrigerate — humidity softens them.
  • Make a double batch; they disappear fast.
