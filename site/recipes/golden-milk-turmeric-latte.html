Golden Milk (Turmeric Latte)

A cozy haldi doodh-inspired warm drink: turmeric, cinnamon, and ginger in softly sweetened milk, with black pepper to wake up the curcumin.

10 min total (5 prep + 5 cook)2 servingseasy difficultyIndian · drink

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk of choice (dairy, oat, almond, or coconut)
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger (or 1 tsp fresh, grated)
  • a generous pinch of freshly ground black pepper
  • 1-2 tsp honey or maple syrup, to taste
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract, optional
  • 1 tbsp coconut cream, optional for body

Nutrition per serving (~ est.)

~140Calories

Instructions

  1. Whisk everything except the sweetener in a small saucepan — turmeric stains, so use a whisk and a pan you don't mind tinting.
  2. Warm over medium-low, whisking, until steaming and lightly frothed, 3-5 minutes — don't boil, or the milk can scorch and the spices turn bitter.
  3. Strain if you used fresh ginger, sweeten to taste, and pour into mugs. Dust with extra cinnamon.

Notes & Substitutions

  • The black pepper and a little fat (from milk or coconut cream) are traditional and functional: both improve curcumin absorption.
  • Weekday shortcut: whisk the dry spices + honey into a paste (it keeps 1 week refrigerated); stir a spoonful into hot milk.
  • Turmeric settles — give it a stir as you sip.
  • Iced version: shake the paste with cold milk over ice.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Best fresh; keep warm up to 30 minutes or refrigerate 1 day and reheat gently, whisking.
  • The spiced paste shortcut keeps 1 week refrigerated.
  • Rinse the pot and cups promptly — turmeric stains set over time.
