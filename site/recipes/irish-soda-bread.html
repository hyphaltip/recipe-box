Brown Irish Soda Bread

Ireland's everyday loaf: four ingredients, no yeast, no proofing, no kneading — the baking soda and buttermilk do all the lifting in the time it takes the oven to heat. Dense, wheaty, faintly tangy, and at its best still warm with butter.

50 min total (10 prep + 40 cook)10 servingseasy difficultyIrish · quickbread

Ingredients

  • 400 g / 3.5 cups whole wheat (wholemeal) flour
  • 100 g / 3/4 cup + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 500 ml / 2 cups buttermilk (see note for a quick homemade version)
  • 2 tbsp rolled oats, for the top (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 220°C / 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment. Whisk the flours, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl — breaking up every clump of soda matters, an unmixed pocket tastes metallic.
  2. Pour in the buttermilk and stir with a wooden spoon or your hand just until a soft, shaggy dough comes together, about 30 seconds. It should be wetter than it looks manageable — a dry soda bakes up crumbly and hard. Add a splash more buttermilk only if dry flour remains.
  3. Turn onto a lightly floured counter and pat into a 5 cm / 2-inch-tall round. Handle it as little as possible — every extra knead tightens the crumb.
  4. Transfer to the sheet. Cut a deep cross across the top with a sharp knife, almost through to the base — tradition says it lets the fairies out; practically, it helps the thick middle bake through. Sprinkle with the oats.
  5. Bake 15 minutes, then reduce to 200°C / 400°F and bake 25 minutes more, until dark golden and the base sounds hollow when knocked.
  6. Cool on a rack at least 45 minutes before slicing — soda bread straight from the oven is doughy inside, and the crumb sets as it cools.

Notes & Substitutions

  • No buttermilk: stir 1 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar into 475 ml / 2 cups whole milk and let it sit 10 minutes until slightly thickened.
  • The 80/20 wholemeal-to-white ratio is the traditional brown soda balance; going 100% wholemeal makes a dense, wholesome doorstop of a loaf (not an insult in this case).
  • Fold in 1 tbsp sugar and 1 cup raisins for a sweet "spotted dog" version — serve with sharp cheddar.
  • The dough can also go into a loaf pan: bake at the same temperature for 10 minutes longer and check for a hollow base.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Soda bread has no fat and keeps only 2 days wrapped at room temperature — it's meant to be baked and eaten.
  • Freeze the whole loaf or slices up to 2 months; toast slices from frozen.
  • Day-two soda bread is prime toast, bread-and-butter, or the base for fried eggs.
