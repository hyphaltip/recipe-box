Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken Noodle Bowls

Bún-style rice noodle bowls: charred lemongrass chicken over cool vermicelli and herbs, drowned in bright nước chấm dressing. Fresh, herby, and endlessly refreshing.

40 min total (25 prep + 15 cook)4 servingsmedium difficultyVietnamese · lunch

Ingredients

  • 500 g / 1 lb boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, tender cores minced (about 3 tbsp)
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1.5 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp white or black pepper
  • 300 g / 10 oz rice vermicelli
  • Dressing (nước chấm):
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 bird's eye chili, thinly sliced, optional
  • Bowls:
  • 1 Persian or half English cucumber, ribboned
  • 2 carrots, julienned
  • 200 g / 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • a big handful each of mint and cilantro
  • 3 tbsp roasted peanuts, crushed
  • lime wedges, to serve

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken with the lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, sugar, turmeric, and pepper; marinate 20 minutes (or overnight).
  2. Stir the dressing ingredients until the sugar dissolves; set aside.
  3. Cook the vermicelli per the package (usually 3-4 minutes in boiling water), drain, and rinse under cold water until the strands are cool and slippery.
  4. Sear the chicken in a lightly oiled hot pan in a single layer until charred at the edges and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side.
  5. Build the bowls: lettuce, noodles, cucumber, carrots, chicken, and a forest of herbs. Pour the dressing over generously, top with peanuts, and serve with lime wedges — toss everything before eating.

Notes & Substitutions

  • The dish lives on herbs and dressing — don't skimp on either.
  • Vegan: swap in lemongrass tofu (pressed, cubed, tossed in the same marinade) and vegan fish sauce.
  • Low-carb: replace noodles with extra lettuce, cucumber, and bean sprouts.
  • The chicken also grills beautifully on skewers.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Components keep 3-4 days refrigerated in separate containers; dress and assemble just before eating so the herbs stay perky.
  • Marinated raw chicken: cook within 24 hours or freeze 2 months.
  • The dressing keeps 1 week refrigerated — make extra.
