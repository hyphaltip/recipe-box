Mediterranean Lentil & Sweet Potato Soup

A brothy, herb-scented pot of brown lentils and sweet potato finished with wilted spinach, lemon, and good olive oil — weeknight comfort with a Mediterranean pantry.

50 min total (15 prep + 35 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyMediterranean · dinner

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried sage
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 200 g / 1 cup dried brown or green lentils, rinsed
  • 500 g / 1 lb sweet potato, peeled and cut into 1.5 cm / 1/2 in cubes
  • 400 g / 14 oz can diced tomatoes
  • 1.4 L / 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 3 cups baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat; cook the onion, carrot, and celery with a pinch of salt until softened, 6-7 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, oregano, sage, and bay leaf; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the lentils, sweet potato, diced tomatoes, and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, partially covered, until the lentils and sweet potato are tender, 25-30 minutes.
  4. Remove the bay leaf. Stir in the spinach and cook until just wilted, 1-2 minutes.
  5. Off the heat, stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and pepper. Ladle into bowls and finish each with a drizzle of olive oil.

Notes & Substitutions

  • For a nut-free protein boost, stir in a spoonful of tahini per bowl instead of the olive oil drizzle.
  • Swap the spinach for chopped kale or Swiss chard; add it a few minutes earlier since it takes longer to wilt.
  • Short on time: canned lentils (2 cans, drained and rinsed) can replace dried — add them in step 4 with the spinach and simmer just 5-10 minutes.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Keeps 5 days refrigerated in an airtight container; the soup thickens as it sits, so thin with a splash of broth or water when reheating.
  • Freezes well for up to 3 months; add the lemon juice fresh after reheating rather than before freezing.
