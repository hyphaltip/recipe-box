Mediterranean Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs with Potatoes & Olives

Bone-in chicken thighs roast to crisp-skinned, juicy perfection on one pan alongside baby potatoes, tomatoes, and olives — minimal cleanup, maximum weeknight payoff.

55 min total (15 prep + 40 cook)4 servingseasy difficultyMediterranean · dinner

Ingredients

  • 700 g / 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1.5 tsp dried oregano, divided
  • salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 1.4 kg / 3 lb)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 200 g / 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 100 g / 2/3 cup pitted kalamata olives
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced, plus wedges to serve
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 60 g / 1/2 cup crumbled feta, optional

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. Toss the potatoes and onion with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp oregano, salt, and pepper on a large sheet pan. Roast 15 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, pat the chicken thighs dry and season generously with salt, pepper, the remaining oregano, and the garlic; toss with the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil.
  3. Push the potatoes to make room and nestle the chicken, skin-side up, among them. Roast until the chicken skin is deeply golden and the internal temperature reaches 74°C / 165°F, 25-30 minutes.
  4. Scatter the cherry tomatoes and olives over the pan for the final 8-10 minutes of roasting, just until the tomatoes blister.
  5. Remove from the oven, squeeze the lemon juice over everything, and scatter with lemon zest, parsley, and feta if using.

Notes & Substitutions

  • For extra-crisp skin, run the pan under the broiler for the last 2-3 minutes, watching closely.
  • Swap the potatoes for quartered fingerling or a halved small cauliflower head for a lower-carb version.
  • Skip the feta for dairy-free; it's naturally gluten-free as written.
  • No bone-in thighs: boneless skinless thighs work too — start them with the tomatoes and olives partway through, since they cook faster and won't need the same time to render fat.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 4 days.
  • Reheat in a 190°C / 375°F oven to keep the skin crisp rather than the microwave.
  • Freezes well up to 3 months; the potatoes soften slightly on thawing but still taste great.
