Misir Wot (Ethiopian Red Lentils)

Ethiopia's beloved red lentil stew — soft lentils in a deep berbere-spiced sauce. Fast-cooking, vegan, and meant to be scooped up with injera.

45 min total (10 prep + 35 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyEthiopian · dinner

Ingredients

  • 400 g / 2 cups red lentils, rinsed until the water runs clear
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2-3 tbsp berbere spice blend, to taste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g / 14 oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 750 ml / 3 cups vegetable broth or water
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • injera or steamed rice, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a pot over medium; cook the onion until soft and translucent, 6-8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute, then add the berbere and cumin. Stir constantly for 30-60 seconds — the spices should smell toasty, not burnt (lower the heat if they darken fast).
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook 2-3 minutes, mashing them into the spice base.
  4. Add the lentils and broth; bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until the lentils have collapsed into a thick, porridge-like stew, 25-30 minutes. Add water if it sticks.
  5. Season with salt and simmer 2 minutes more. Serve on injera, family style, with the stew ladled in the middle for scooping.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Berbere is the soul of the dish — find it at African markets or online, or blend your own (dried chilies, paprika, fenugreek, cardamom, allspice, cloves).
  • Heat control: start at 2 tbsp berbere; some blends are fierce.
  • Traditional wot uses niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter) — for a plant-based kitchen, the neutral oil version is standard everyday fare.
  • Wilt in spinach at the end, or top with avocado slices.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Refrigerate up to 5 days; freezes up to 3 months.
  • Deepens overnight like all good wot — reheat gently and loosen with a splash of water.
  • Leftovers make a fantastic wrap filling or baked-potato topper.
