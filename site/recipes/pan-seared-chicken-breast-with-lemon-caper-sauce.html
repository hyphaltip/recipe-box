Pan-Seared Chicken Breast with Lemon-Caper Sauce

Thin-pounded chicken breast seared until golden, finished with a bright lemon-caper pan sauce — a 30-minute skillet dinner that tastes like more effort than it is.

30 min total (10 prep + 20 cook)4 servingseasy difficultyFrench · dinner

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 680 g / 1.5 lb total)
  • salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 240 ml / 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice, plus lemon slices to garnish
  • 2 tbsp capers, drained
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Place each chicken breast between sheets of plastic wrap and pound to an even 1.5 cm / 1/2 in thickness; season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken until golden and cooked through, 4-5 minutes per side (internal temperature 74°C / 165°F). Transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
  3. Reduce heat to medium; melt 1 tbsp butter in the same skillet and cook the garlic until fragrant, 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the broth and lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits; simmer until reduced by about a third, 3-4 minutes. Stir in the capers.
  5. Off the heat, swirl in the remaining tbsp butter until glossy. Return the chicken to the pan to coat in the sauce, then plate and scatter with parsley and lemon slices.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Dairy-free: use olive oil in place of both butter additions — the sauce will be a touch less glossy but still bright.
  • No capers on hand: chopped green olives or a extra squeeze of lemon both work.
  • Pounding the chicken thin is what keeps it juicy in a quick sear — don't skip it, especially with larger breasts.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Refrigerate in an airtight container up to 4 days; reheat gently in a covered skillet over low heat to avoid drying out.
  • The sauce doesn't freeze as well as the chicken — freeze the seared chicken alone (up to 3 months) and make a fresh sauce when reheating.
