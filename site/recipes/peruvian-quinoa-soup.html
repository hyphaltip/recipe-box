Peruvian Quinoa Soup (Sopa de Quinua)

An Andean high-altitude classic — a golden, restorative broth of quinoa, potatoes, and vegetables. Humble ingredients, deeply satisfying bowl.

50 min total (15 prep + 35 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyPeruvian · lunch

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika (ají panca or aji amarillo paste if you can find it)
  • 200 g / 1 cup quinoa, rinsed well
  • 1.5 liters / 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 400 g / 3 medium Yukon Gold potatoes, cubed
  • 400 g / 14 oz can peas (or 1 cup frozen peas or shelled fava beans)
  • 200 g / 2 cups baby spinach
  • a handful of cilantro or mint, chopped
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1.5 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • avocado slices and lime wedges, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium; soften the onion and carrots with a pinch of salt, 6-7 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, and paprika; stir until fragrant, 1 minute.
  3. Add the quinoa and stir 2 minutes to toast — this keeps it nutty instead of soggy.
  4. Pour in the broth, add the potatoes, and simmer until nearly tender, 15 minutes.
  5. Add the quinoa and simmer until the grains unfurl into little spirals and the potatoes are fully tender, 12-14 minutes.
  6. Stir in the peas and spinach; cook 2-3 minutes. Finish with lime juice, cilantro, and salt. Serve with avocado and lime wedges.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Toasting the rinsed quinoa in the oil is the flavor trick — don't skip the rinse (it washes off natural bitter saponins).
  • For a heartier pot, stir in shredded rotisserie chicken with the peas (no longer vegan).
  • Aji amarillo paste (Latin markets) swaps in for the paprika for a gently fruity Peruvian heat.
  • Blend one ladle of the soup and stir it back in for a creamier body.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Refrigerate up to 4 days; freezes up to 3 months.
  • Quinoa drinks liquid — reheat with extra broth until soup-like again.
  • Make a double batch; it's a perfect thermos lunch.
