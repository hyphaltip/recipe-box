Skillet Apple Pie with Whole Wheat Crust

A deep-dish skillet pie with a nutty whole wheat crust and a filling sweetened mostly by the apples themselves — less sugar, more apple, all the cinnamon comfort.

80 min total (30 prep + 50 cook)8 servingsmedium difficultyAmerican · pie

Ingredients

  • Whole wheat crust: 150 g / 1 cup whole wheat or white whole wheat flour
  • 150 g / 1 cup + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 170 g / 12 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 4-6 tbsp ice water
  • 1.3 kg / 3 lb apples (half Honeycrisp or Fuji, half Granny Smith), peeled and sliced 6 mm / 1/4 inch thick
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp cornstarch
  • 1.5 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp butter, in small dots
  • 1 egg, beaten with 1 tsp water, and 1 tbsp coarse or plain sugar for sprinkling

Instructions

  1. Make the crust: pulse the flours, sugar, and salt in a food processor; add the butter and pulse to pea-sized crumbs. Add ice water 1 tbsp at a time until the dough just holds when squeezed. Split 60/40, wrap, and chill at least 45 minutes.
  2. Roll the larger piece into a 30 cm / 12 inch round and drape it into a 25 cm / 10 inch oven-safe skillet, letting the excess hang over. Chill.
  3. Toss the apples with sugar, lemon juice, cornstarch, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt; pile into the crust (it will tower — it cooks down), dot with the butter.
  4. Roll the smaller piece and lay it over (or cut into lattice strips); press the edges together and fold the overhang inward to make a rim. Brush with egg wash, sprinkle with sugar, and cut 3 steam vents.
  5. Bake at 190 °C / 375 °F for 45-55 minutes, until the crust is deep gold and the filling bubbles thickly through the vents. Tent loosely with foil if the crust browns too fast.
  6. Cool at least 1 hour in the skillet — the filling sets as it cools. Serve barely warm or at room temperature.

Notes & Substitutions

  • A skillet conducts heat better than a pie plate, so the bottom crust actually bakes through — the classic reason for skillet pie.
  • Three tablespoons of sugar for three pounds of apples works because the apples carry it; taste your apples and add 1 tbsp more only if they're sharply tart.
  • Cornstarch keeps the filling clear and glossy; flour makes it cloudy but works in a pinch (use 1.5 tbsp).
  • Serve with plain Greek yogurt instead of ice cream — honestly good.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Room temperature 2 days, loosely covered; refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • The crust can be made 2 days ahead and held in the fridge.
  • Reheat slices 10 minutes at 175 °C / 350 °F to re-crisp the bottom.
