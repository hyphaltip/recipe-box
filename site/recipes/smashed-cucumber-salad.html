Smashed Cucumber Salad (拍黄瓜)

The Chinese cold side dish that makes cucumbers exciting: smashed into rough, craggy pieces that grab dressing, then tossed with garlic, black vinegar, sesame oil, and a whisper of chili. Five ingredients, fifteen minutes, endlessly snackable.

15 min total (10 prep + 5 cook)4 servingseasy difficultyChinese · side

Ingredients

  • 2 English cucumbers (or 4 Persian), about 600 g / 1.3 lb total
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 1.5 tbsp soy sauce (tamari for gluten-free)
  • 1 tbsp Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang) or rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated to a paste
  • 1 tsp chili crisp or chili flakes, optional
  • 1/4 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn, optional
  • a handful of cilantro and toasted sesame seeds, to finish

Instructions

  1. Lay the cucumbers on a cutting board and smack them hard with the flat of a chef's knife or a rolling pin until they split and crack — the craggy surfaces are the whole trick.
  2. Cut into rough 4 cm / 1.5 inch chunks. Toss with the salt and leave in a colander 10 minutes to draw out water; pat dry.
  3. Whisk the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, chili, and Sichuan peppercorn until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Toss the cucumbers with the dressing; finish with cilantro and sesame seeds. Serve cold, within the hour.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Salting first is what keeps it crisp and punchy instead of watery — don't skip the 10 minutes.
  • Black vinegar brings smoky depth; rice vinegar makes it cleaner and sharper. Both are correct, different moods.
  • Add-ins that work: julienned carrot, thin-sliced shallot, a handful of crushed roasted peanuts, or cubed avocado (goes creamy).
  • Make it a light lunch: toss with cubed cold tofu or shredded chicken.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Best within 1 hour of dressing — cucumbers keep draining as they sit.
  • Dressed leftovers keep 1 day refrigerated (softer but still tasty).
  • The dressing keeps a week in the fridge; smash fresh cucumbers to order.
