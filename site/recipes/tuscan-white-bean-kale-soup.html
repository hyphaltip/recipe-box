Tuscan White Bean & Kale Soup

A ribollita-inspired pantry soup: creamy cannellini beans, hearty lacinato kale, and a parmesan-scented broth that tastes slow-cooked in half an hour.

45 min total (15 prep + 30 cook)6 servingseasy difficultyItalian · lunch

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary (or 1 sprig fresh)
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes, plus more to serve
  • 2 x 400 g / 14 oz cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 400 g / 14 oz can diced tomatoes
  • 1 liter / 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 parmesan rind, optional
  • 200 g / 1 bunch lacinato kale, stemmed and roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • salt and black pepper, to taste
  • olive oil and grated parmesan, to serve

Instructions

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat; add the onion, carrot, and celery with a pinch of salt and cook until softened, 6-8 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic, rosemary, and pepper flakes; cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Add the beans, tomatoes, broth, and parmesan rind; bring to a simmer.
  4. Simmer 15 minutes, then mash about a quarter of the beans against the side of the pot to thicken the broth.
  5. Stir in the kale and cook until wilted and tender, 5-7 minutes.
  6. Remove the rind and rosemary sprig; finish with lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Serve with a thread of olive oil and grated parmesan.

Notes & Substitutions

  • Vegan: skip the parmesan rind and topping; add 1 tbsp white miso paste at the end for savory depth.
  • Lighter swap: this is already lean — the olive oil is the main fat; 1 tbsp works fine if you prefer.
  • Kale substitutes: chard, escarole, or savoy cabbage (add 2-3 extra minutes).
  • Blend half the soup for a creamier texture without dairy.

Storage & Make-Ahead

  • Refrigerate up to 4 days; freezes up to 3 months.
  • The soup thickens as it sits — loosen with a splash of broth or water when reheating.
  • Prep the soffritto (onion, carrot, celery) up to 3 days ahead.
